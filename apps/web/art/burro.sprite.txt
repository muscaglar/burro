# Burro is a rabbit: a wild one, brown and grey, with a pale belly and a pale scut. He holds
# nothing, wears nothing and has no smile. His eye is two pixels. There are three drawings of
# him: he sits, he waits and he hops. A strip holds what he does where he rests, which is
# where he sits, and is made of that drawing. Another holds the soil that flies as he hops,
# and lies over his hop. He asks nothing and nothing stops him, so nothing is drawn of him
# asking, and no button of his.
#
# Every drawing of him is on a canvas 24 wide and 30 high, and a frame of a strip is one
# canvas. He stands on row 28, counted from nought, and so does his burrow: so he never jumps
# as one drawing gives way to the next. test/art.test.ts holds the three to it, and the test
# beside the part that draws him holds the strips.
#
# His fur is fur, lit in sand and shaded in shade. What is pale of him is page: his belly, his
# muzzle and his scut. His outline and his eye are ink. His burrow is earth, its clods are
# shade, and the dark of its mouth is ink. The soil he throws up from it is earth and shade
# too. He, his burrow and that soil are all that is brown on the website: no other drawing
# holds fur or earth.
#
# The light is from the top left in every frame, whichever way he faces.
# The text is the drawing, one character a pixel. Change it here, then run `npm run gen:art`.
