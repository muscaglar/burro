# The picture at each end of a gauge: one picture at each end, and the two are opposites. Of a
# vibe that runs between two ends that have names, what each end is: houses and flats, calm
# and buzzy, newer and historic. Of a vibe that runs one way, little of the thing at its low
# end and much of it at its high end.
#
# Neither end is the better one. A picture says what there is, and never that it is good or
# bad: no tick and no cross, no smile and no frown, and no red for the one and green for the
# other. None pictures a person: a vibe that counts who lived somewhere is a page of a
# register, with little written on it or much.
#
# A picture is named for its vibe, by the id the service gives with every underscore a hyphen,
# and for its end: end-leafy-low and end-leafy-high for the vibe leafy. An end with no picture
# of its own has end-blank. Every one is 16 square, as a thing of a search is, so one may take
# the place of another, and each stands on its last row.
#
# An end may be drawn a second way, under its name with -b after it, where it was not plain
# which picture says the end best. src/components/kit/Ends/look.ts chooses which way is drawn,
# in one line. A second way stands for a first, and is held to all that a first is held to.
# The text is the drawing, one character a pixel. Change it here, then run `npm run gen:art`.
