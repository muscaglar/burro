# The things of a search: the small drawing that stands beside the words of a chip. Town Map
# drew thirteen, and they are taken, each under the name of what it stands for. The rest are
# drawn in its manner: an outline of ink, flat colour, one light from the top left.
#
# A thing is named thing-ID, by the id the service gives with every underscore a hyphen:
# thing-leafy for the vibe leafy, thing-family-green for the family green. A vibe with no
# drawing of its own takes its family's, and what has no family that is drawn takes thing-plain.
# What is no vibe: thing-journey, thing-budget, thing-tenure, thing-home, thing-area,
# thing-alike and thing-usual.
#
# A vibe whose id begins as the thing of a family does is named for a vibe outright:
# thing-vibe-family-amenities for the vibe family_amenities. Named thing-family-amenities, it
# was taken for the thing of a family, which no vibe may be drawn by, and was drawn nowhere.
#
# A group of measures that the settings show apart from the families of vibes is drawn as a
# family is, by the dimension the service gives its measures: thing-family-brands,
# thing-family-air-noise and thing-family-crime.
#
# Every thing is 16 square, so one may take the place of another. No word and no figure is
# drawn on one. None pictures a person: a vibe of the family who_lives_there is a page of a
# register, ruled, and nothing else.
#
# Town Map's boot and purse were brown leather, and its wood was brown. Brown is the rabbit's
# and his burrow's, and nothing else holds it: each has another colour here.
# The text is the drawing, one character a pixel. Change it here, then run `npm run gen:art`.
