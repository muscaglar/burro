# The tiles of Town Map's map that the website takes: the four of meadow, of which the ground of
# every page is made, and the two of water. Each is 16 square and lies edge to edge with any
# other: nothing drawn on a tile touches its edge.
#
# A grain on the land or on the water is a grain. It says nothing of the place it lies on.
# The text is the drawing, one character a pixel. Change it here, then run `npm run gen:art`.
