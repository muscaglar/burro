# The little town of an area: a few buildings and trees on a plot of meadow, built by rule
# from where the area sits on four vibes. One vibe is its trees, one the height of its
# buildings, one its lit windows and one its roofs. src/lib/town/ holds the rule, and says
# which piece is drawn where.
#
# It is a drawing made from four figures and is no picture of a place. So it holds nothing
# the four do not: no church, no river, no landmark, no shop, no car and nobody at all.
#
# The pieces are Town Map's, drawn again so that each part of a town holds one vibe and no
# other: a wall is the same wall under any roof, and is lit the same whatever its roof. Town
# Map's brown is turned to shade, since brown is the rabbit's.
#
# What is not known is left blank. Where a tree would stand, and where a building would, the
# place is marked out on the meadow by one line of shade, whole: a ring, and a rectangle. A
# wall whose roof is not known has none, and is closed by one line of shade from corner to
# corner. Windows of which nothing is known are dark, in ink. None of these is the least of
# its scale, which is always drawn: one tree, a house, one window lit.
#
# No mark is drawn in dots or in dashes. They were, as Town Map drew them, and a person who
# walked the website did not know what an edge in pieces was for.
#
# A drawing of several frames is a strip: its frames stand side by side, and each has a clear
# column at either hand, so that nothing of one frame shows at the edge of the next.
#
# The text is the drawing, one character a pixel. Change it here, then run `npm run gen:art`.
