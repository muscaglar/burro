# The box, the button and the marks of the look, as Town Map drew them.
#
# A frame is cut in nine and shown as a border image. It says its cut as border-image-slice is
# written: from the top, the right, the foot, the left. The light is from the top left, so a
# shadow falls down and to the right and nowhere else.
#
# A button pressed is drawn on the canvas of the button up, with its cut. The button itself,
# which is what takes the press, never moves and never changes size: only its face steps, one
# art pixel down and one to the right, into the place of its shadow. It is named for the button
# up, with -down after it.
#
# Town Map held its brown as the shade of amber and of poppy. Brown is the rabbit's and his
# burrow's, and nothing else holds it: so that shade is poppy under amber, and shade under poppy.
# The text is the drawing, one character a pixel. Change it here, then run `npm run gen:art`.
