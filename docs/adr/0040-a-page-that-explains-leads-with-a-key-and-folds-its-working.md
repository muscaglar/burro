# 0040. A page that explains leads with a key, and folds its working

Status: accepted, 2026-09-26. The founder asked for it, once they had walked the website. What leads each page, what is folded and under which name were set in the building, and are the founder's to overturn. What stays in sight is not: a licence or a promise asks for each, and the second table of the decision says which. **Amended that evening, once the founder had walked the website a second time**: three pages explain, where there were four, since the statement of accessibility is gone, and that a vibe is a rough guide is no longer among what stays in sight, since no page says it. See "Amended, 2026-09-26", below. **Amended on 2026-09-27, once the founder had walked the website a third time**: the key holds what was drawn for that walk, and says what the two pictures of a gauge mean and what "approx data" means. Where the two pictures of every vibe stand was decided as it was built, and is the founder's to overturn. See "Amended, 2026-09-27", below.

## Context

- Three pages explain the website: the vibes, the methods and the sources. Each is built from what the service says of a release, so that none can disagree with the engine.
- Each said all that is true of its subject, at full length. In the look the page of vibes was 12,759 px on a desktop and 27,524 on a phone, the methods 20,659 and 49,215, and the sources 2,594 and 6,469. How a band is drawn, and what a town is built of, stood at the foot of the page of vibes.
- The founder walked through the website, and wrote: "We dont need as much information in the methods, vibes and sources, this should be simplified a fair bit. A key to the visuals/icons is valuable, and should be expanded. Simplify the other sections on the vibe page." And: "Methods is too much informtion, and probably of no value to a user". And: "The what a town is built of is also useful, mix in with the visuals/icons section."
- Burro shows its working: every figure traces to a fact with a source and a date (rule 6), and a vibe says what it is made of and what it cannot see.
- The licences of the data ask that a source is credited, some of them in words of their own, and some publishers ask that a statement stands wherever their credit is shown.

## Decision

**A page that explains leads with what a visitor needs, in words they can follow, and folds its working under that, whole. Nothing is taken out of a page: what is not in sight is one press away. What a licence or a promise asks to be seen stays in sight.**

| Matter | What is so |
|---|---|
| The key | The first thing on the page of vibes, under the name "Key to the drawings". It holds every drawing and mark that a visitor meets on the website, each at the size it is met, beside a sentence or two that say what it means. It is laid out by where a drawing is met. The things of a search: the small drawing of each vibe, of what else can be asked for, a thing in outline, which counts for nothing, the gauge, which says how much a thing counts, and the cross that takes a thing out. Where an area sits on a vibe: the five steps and the peg, in each state they are drawn in. In a search and on a result: the carrot where Burro asks, the rank on its pennant, the key that opens a source, the arrow of what opens and closes, and the edge of amber of a notice and of poppy of a fault. On the map: the five greens, land with no match, dots, lines, water and a pin. All of it is in sight, and nothing of it is behind a press |
| What a town is built of | Inside the key, and not after it: its trees, the height of its buildings, its lit windows, its roofs, and what a blank means ([0034](0034-the-town-of-an-area-is-drawn-by-rule-and-is-no-picture-of-the-place.md), as amended) |
| What the key draws | Nothing new. Every drawing of it is one the website draws already, from the same picture and the same tokens. Nothing of the rabbit is in it: he is drawn where a person searches |
| A vibe | After the key, and far shorter than it was: its drawing, its name, what it means in a sentence or two, its two ends where it has two, and the small map of the city coloured by it |
| The working of a vibe | One fold under each vibe, closed, named for what a person would ask: "How Burro works this out". It holds what goes into the vibe, with the share, the period and the source of each part, what the vibe cannot see, and where it is found band by band. Nothing of it is lost. How an area is placed on a vibe is true of every vibe, and is one fold at the foot of the page |
| The methods | A short, plain account of how Burro finds areas for a person, which somebody reads in a minute and could tell a friend, in four paragraphs: what it looks at, how it ranks, what it will not do, and that every figure has its source. It states no figure. Under it one fold, closed, holds all of the rest as it was, and each part of it keeps the id that another page leads to it by. The key of a town no longer stands there |
| The sources | One list, in one box. Of each source, in sight: its name, who published it, its licence, the way to the publisher, and its credit. What Burro uses the source for folds under it, with when its copy was taken |
| A fold | The browser's own, so that it opens with scripts off. A link that names what a fold holds opens it, so that a result, the search box and the page of an area lead where they led |
| A heading | Only where it helps somebody find something ([0038](0038-the-words-of-the-website-are-written-for-a-person-who-has-never-seen-burro.md)) |

**What stays in sight, and what asks for it.**

| In sight | Asked for by |
|---|---|
| The credit of every source, word for word where its licence gives the words | The licence of the source. No dataset is shown but as its licence allows (rule 1) |
| What a publisher asks to be said wherever its credit is shown | The terms of that publisher. It stands under the credit, in the service's words |
| That a vibe is a rough guide, with the sentence that says why | [0013](0013-vibes-are-the-centre.md): a vibe that is less sure than the rest says so wherever it is shown, and never behind a press. It says so beside its name in the key too. *Overturned on the evening of 2026-09-26: no page says it, in sight or folded* |
| That a vibe counts recorded crime | [0006](0006-rank-places-not-residents.md) and [0013](0013-vibes-are-the-centre.md): such a vibe says so on its face |
| The banner that says the data is made up, and the banner of a preview | Rule 13, and [0017](0017-a-preview-says-it-is-one.md) |

What was weighed, and put aside:

| Way | Why not |
|---|---|
| The tables of the methods taken out of the page | The founder holds them of no value to a visitor. They stay, folded: Burro shows its working, and a person who doubts a figure finds how it was made |
| What a vibe cannot see left in sight under every vibe | It is honest, and it is long. It stays, in the fold of its vibe, in the service's words |
| The credit of a source folded with the rest of what is said of it | Its licence asks that it is seen |
| Drawings made for the key | A key is of what a visitor meets. A drawing they meet nowhere else would need a key of its own |

## Consequences

- **Each page is far shorter before anything is opened.** Measured on 2026-09-26, of the website as it stands: the page of vibes is 7,251 px on a desktop and 14,416 on a phone, where it was 12,759 and 27,524 before this decision. The methods are 1,106 and 1,845, where they were 20,659 and 49,215, and the sources 900 and 1,022, where they were 2,594 and 6,469. [The design](../design/web.md), section 4.2, has it.
- **Every address that led into one of these pages leads there still**: to how a journey is timed, to what a word for how sure a cost is means and to how an area is named, on the methods; to a vibe by its id; to a source by its id. Where what it leads to is folded, the fold opens.
- **The key is new site copy**, in `apps/web/src/content/about.ts`, and is the first thing a visitor reads of what the drawings mean. It is written as [the guide to the words](../design/words.md) asks. It gives no verdict: it says that a peg further along means more of a thing, and not that the area is better.
- What holds it, in `apps/web/src/components/`: `VibesList/Key.test.tsx`, with `test_it_is_laid_out_by_where_a_drawing_is_met_and_what_a_town_is_built_of_is_inside_it`, `test_all_of_it_is_in_sight_and_nothing_of_it_is_behind_a_press`, `test_a_drawing_is_shown_at_its_own_size_and_never_stretched` and `test_the_key_gives_no_verdict_and_says_that_more_is_not_better`. `About/Fold.test.tsx`, with `test_it_is_the_browsers_own_and_is_closed_until_it_is_pressed_so_that_it_opens_with_scripts_off` and `test_every_fold_that_holds_what_the_address_names_is_opened_and_it_is_brought_into_sight`. And the tests beside `VibesList`, `MethodsTables` and `SourceList`.
- **The words of the service stand beside it as they came**: the meaning of a vibe, its name and the names of its ends. Which of them read badly is the founder's to weigh ([0038](0038-the-words-of-the-website-are-written-for-a-person-who-has-never-seen-burro.md)).
- **The statement of accessibility is a page that explains, and is not folded.** The founder asked nothing of it. What it says is brought up to what the website now does, and must stay true. *The founder asked for it to go on the evening of 2026-09-26, and it went ([0042](0042-the-website-makes-no-claim-of-a-standard-of-accessibility.md)).*

## Amended, 2026-09-26

The founder walked the website a second time, that evening, and wrote two things that bear on the pages that explain: "get rid of the accessibility tab. Overkill", and "remove the concept of rough guide, we don't want to pass this on to a user".

| Matter | What was decided | What stands now |
|---|---|---|
| Which pages explain | The vibes, the methods and the sources, with the statement of accessibility beside them, which was not folded | The vibes, the methods and the sources. The statement is gone, with its link in the name board and in the foot ([0042](0042-the-website-makes-no-claim-of-a-standard-of-accessibility.md)) |
| What stays in sight of a vibe | That it counts recorded crime, and that it is a rough guide, with the sentence that says why | That it counts recorded crime. That a vibe is less sure than the rest is said on no page ([0013](0013-vibes-are-the-centre.md), as amended) |
| What the key holds | Every drawing and mark that a visitor meets. As the website was mended that afternoon it opens with its parts by name, each a link to its part. It held a row for the two marks of the button beside the rabbit, and the label of a rough guide beside the name of its vibe | Every drawing and mark that a visitor meets. The row of the button went with the button, and the label of a rough guide with what it said. **The row of the carrot was written again that night.** The carrot lay where Burro asked, and its row said that Burro asks where it is not sure and adds nothing until a person chooses, which was untrue of a page that asks nothing. A carrot is met still beside the choice in hand in a list, and the row says so. The key says too what Burro does with a sentence, beside the cross of a chip, and what it never takes for a person |
| How words are handled | A part of the methods, inside their fold, which the search box led to | The same part, under the same id. The search box leads nowhere, and the foot of every page leads there, by the word "Privacy" ([0023](0023-what-is-typed-goes-as-typed-and-people-are-told.md), as amended). An address that names it opens the fold that holds it, as before |
| Everything else of this record | | As it was: a page leads with what a visitor needs and folds its working whole, a fold is the browser's own, the credit of every source is in sight, and a drawing that is added to the website is added to the key |

What it leaves in sight is asked for by a licence or a promise still. Of the promises, one was the founder's to take back, and they took it back.

The key says what the two pictures of a vibe mean: little of the thing and much of it, or, of a vibe that runs between two opposites, what each end is. **It says that neither end is the better one** |
