**What it gives up.**

- **A person who reads a result and presses nothing sees no source and no date.** They see a fit, where the area sits on each thing they asked for, and the figure of a trade-off. - **The credit of every source is in sight on the page of sources**, word for word where its licence gives the words, as it was ([0040](0040-a-page-that-explains-leads-with-a-key-and-folds-its-working.md)). What follows is of what stands with a figure where the figure is shown.
- **The licence registry asks, of many sources, that something stands beside every figure made from them, or wherever one is shown.** Of three it holds a mark for it, `attribution_beside_figures`, and a release carries the publisher's own credit to every fact that cites them: Transport for London's stations, its bus routes and stops, and the Department for Transport's counts of traffic, with the note the department asks for on how far they are to be relied on. Of others its conditions ask it in words: the date or the period of a figure, of what homes sell and let for, of land use, of gardens, of surgeries, of pharmacies and of places to eat; the credit of two publishers wherever what homes sold for is shown; and a caution wherever recorded crime or a figure of the census is. Until the third walk each stood behind the key that followed the figure, one press from it, and that was read as beside it. Each now stands behind the same key, under the working of the result. **No publisher's terms were read again for this, and no condition of the registry was changed.** Whether under the working is where each asks for it is the founder's to settle before a release of London is served to this website. The made-up city has one source, which asks nothing.
- **How much of a vibe could be measured is no longer in sight.** "Approx data" says that a band rests on part of what it needs, and not how large a part. A band needs 60 in 100 of what its vibe counts, so the part is never less than that.
- **Why a fit is not whole is no longer in sight.** The same two words stand beside a fit that is based on some of what counts, beside one whose journeys count for nothing, and beside one that a firm limit could not be held against. Which it is, is under the working. Of the three the last is the gravest: a person called a limit firm, it was not applied to the area, and the result says so in two words that say the same of any rough figure.
- **Why an area fits is no longer said where the area is first shown.** A person who wants it opens the working.
- **One thing of a result is still said at length.** Where what an area gives up is a vibe whose band is not whole, the sentence of the trade-off ends in what the band rests on, since the sentence is the service's and no sentence of the service was changed. To take it from a result the service would say such a vibe in fewer words.
- **Which way a journey falls against its limit is no longer said under a trade-off.** The sentence of a journey that is over its limit says by how much, and "Over your limit of 30 minutes" stood under it all the same. It was taken out as the result was built, and is the founder's to put back.
- **The two ends of a gauge are not named in sight on a result.** A picture says what an end is, and the key to the drawings says what each picture means. Whether a picture is known at a glance is seen by asking people.
