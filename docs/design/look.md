# The look of the website

Status: true of the website as it was built on 2026-09-26, brought that afternoon to what the founder asked for once they had walked it, brought that evening to what was mended since and to what they asked for on a second walk, that night to what was measured of the website, and on 2026-09-27 to what they asked for on a third walk and to what was measured of the website as it stands since. It is for a person who will change the look: it says what is drawn where, where each thing is changed, how each part of the look is turned down in one line, and what is still open for the founder. What was decided, and why, is in three decision records, each amended once the founder had walked the website: the look ([ADR 0033](../adr/0033-the-website-looks-like-the-map-of-a-gentle-game.md)), the town of an area ([ADR 0034](../adr/0034-the-town-of-an-area-is-drawn-by-rule-and-is-no-picture-of-the-place.md)) and where things stand on the search page ([ADR 0035](../adr/0035-the-search-page-is-one-box-and-the-look-gives-way-to-the-answer.md)). Three more were recorded then: how the words are written ([ADR 0038](../adr/0038-the-words-of-the-website-are-written-for-a-person-who-has-never-seen-burro.md)), that comparing areas is a headline ([ADR 0039](../adr/0039-comparing-areas-is-a-headline-of-the-website-and-nobody-wins-a-comparison.md)), and that a page that explains leads with a key ([ADR 0040](../adr/0040-a-page-that-explains-leads-with-a-key-and-folds-its-working.md)). After the second walk each of the six was amended again, and one more bears on the look: that the website makes no claim of a standard of accessibility, with which the button that stopped the rabbit went ([ADR 0042](../adr/0042-the-website-makes-no-claim-of-a-standard-of-accessibility.md)). [The design of the website](web.md) says what each page holds and what a browser showed, and its section 8 has every token. Every path here is under `apps/web/`, and one that begins with the name of a part, as `ResultList/look.ts` and `kit/Press/Press.module.css` do, is under `src/components/`.

The look is Town Map: the map of a gentle game, drawn in pixels. Every page stands on a meadow. Whatever is read stands in a box of cream. Names are set in a face that is drawn in pixels, and every sentence and every figure in a face that is made to be read. Burro is a rabbit. **Reading comes first, nothing is won, and nothing is drawn that the data does not hold.**

Everything here was seen in one browser, Chrome, at 390 by 844 and at 1440 by 900, on the made-up city. It was seen in still pictures, and measured by software: nobody has watched anything move. What is said "seen" or "measured" in section 3 was seen before the founder walked the website, of the page as it then was. In sections 4 and 5 a figure says when it was measured: as a part was built, that afternoon as the website was mended, on 2026-09-26 of the website as it stood after the second walk, or on 2026-09-27 of the website as it stands, which is the website since the third. [The design of the website](web.md), sections 4.1 and 4.2, has the whole of what was measured of it.

## 3. How the look is turned down

The founder decided that the look is built in full, and said of what a regulation may ask: "we can remove things or downplay things in relation to regulation later if needed". So every part of the look is built to be turned down by itself, in one line where that could be had.

### 3.1 What may have to be toned down, and why

[The page on children](../legal/access-by-children.md) has the concern. The Children's code applies to a service that children are likely to use, and the terms of the model that reads what is typed forbid one. The regulator names "cartoons, animation, music or audio content, incentives for children's participation, digital functionalities such as gamification" among what appeals to children. That page is a draft, and says that to take the game out of the look would not by itself show that children had gone: what follows there is that the model goes off.

### 3.2 Part by part, and the line for each
