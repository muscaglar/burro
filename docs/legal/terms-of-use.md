# Using Burro

Last updated: `[FOUNDER: date of publication]`

These are the terms for using Burro. They are short on purpose. If you do not agree with them, please do not use Burro. How Burro handles what you tell it is in the [privacy notice](privacy-notice.md).

## 1. What Burro is

| | |
|---|---|
| What it does | Burro helps you decide where in London to live. You describe the life you want and name the places you need to reach. Burro ranks named neighbourhoods on a map and shows its working. These terms call a named neighbourhood an area |
| How it ranks | By arithmetic over published data. The same search on the same data gives the same answer |
| What it costs | Nothing |
| What you need | No account |

## 2. What Burro is not

| Burro is not | What that means |
|---|---|
| Advice | It is not financial, legal, mortgage or property advice. It does not know your circumstances |
| A valuation | A range of rents or prices is a guide to what homes cost in an area. It says nothing of any one home |
| A verdict on whether you can afford a place | Burro compares a budget with a range. It does not know your income or your costs |
| A verdict on safety | Figures for recorded crime are counts of what the police recorded, which is not the same as what happened. Burro never calls a place safe or unsafe. Recorded crime counts in a ranking only if you ask for it by name, switch it on, or ask for a vibe that counts it. `[NOT BUILT: one vibe counts it, Gritty. Its page says so]` |
| A judgement of the people who live somewhere | Burro ranks places by what is there. `[NOT BUILT: of the people who live in an area it counts two things, and only when you ask: how old they were and what their households were made of, at the census of 2021. You can ask for more of what a figure counts, and never for fewer of anyone.]` It ranks on nothing else about who lives anywhere |
| A guide to who an area is for | `[NOT BUILT: an area's page shows the statistics office's own figures for country of birth, ethnic group and religion.]` They say who was counted there on one day in 2021. They do not say who is welcome, and nobody may use them to decide that: section 7 |
| A journey planner | A journey time is an estimate, door to door, for a weekday morning. It allows for no closure, strike or delay |
| A place to find a home | Burro shows no home for sale or to rent, and links to none. `[FOUNDER: confirm: Burro takes no payment from anyone who sells or lets homes]` |
| A promise | A high rank means an area fits the settings you gave, on the data Burro holds. It does not mean you will like it |

## 3. The figures

| | |
|---|---|
| Where they come from | Each figure comes from a named publisher. The source and the date are shown with the figure, and the sources page lists every publisher |
| They can be out of date | A publisher may have newer figures than Burro holds. Some figures are years old. The date says how old |
| They can be wrong | A publisher can make a mistake, and so can Burro in working a figure out |
| Some are estimates | A cost says how far it can be trusted: high, medium or low. A low one is modelled |
| Some are missing | Where Burro has no figure, it says so and fills nothing in. The result says how complete it is |
| What to do | Check what matters to you before you decide. Visit the area. Time the journey yourself. Ask the school, the council or the agent |
| If you find a mistake | Tell Burro: section 13 |

## 4. Made-up data, and data that is not finished

Burro can show three kinds of data. Every page says which.

| Kind | How you can tell | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Made up | A banner on every page says that the data is made-up test data, and cannot be closed | The city, the areas, the names and every figure are invented. Nothing shown under that banner is a fact about any real place. Do not rely on it for anything |
| A preview of real data | A banner on every page says that it is a preview for the people who build Burro, and cannot be closed | The figures are of real places, and the data is not finished. What has not been measured is missing, and nothing in it has been approved for the public. If you see one, do not rely on it for anything |
| Finished | No banner | Sections 2 and 3 still apply |

## 5. When a computer reads your words

| | |
|---|---|
| What reads them | Burro's own rules, or a language model run by another company. The notice beside the box says which, before you type |
| It can misread you | The page shows what was understood, directly under the box. Check it, and change any setting that is wrong |
| What is applied without asking you | Only words that are a plain list of what you want. Of any other words Burro applies nothing: it shows what it noticed, and you choose. `[NOT BUILT: nothing a language model reads is applied until you choose it]` |
| What it never does | It never ranks a place, scores one, or describes one from its own knowledge |

## 6. Who may use Burro

`[FOUNDER: You must be 18 or over.]` Burro does not ask your age and does not check it.

## 7. What you agree not to do

| Do not | Why |
|---|---|
| Use Burro to decide about a person: who to let to, sell to, lend to, insure or employ | Burro describes places. It must never be used to choose between people |
| Use Burro to keep people out of an area, or to steer them to one, because of who they are | It is against what Burro is for, and the Equality Act 2010 forbids discrimination by those who provide services and by those who let or sell homes. The rest of this section says what that means if you do either |
| Present Burro's figures as its verdict on a place or on the people who live there | They are other publishers' figures, with their dates |
| Type in details about another person that they would not want shared | Burro does not keep them. A company that runs a model may: see the privacy notice |
| Send requests by a program, in bulk, or faster than a person would | Burro is free and small. `[NOT BUILT: Burro may slow or refuse requests that look automated]` |
| Try to break Burro, get round a limit, or reach what is not meant for you | |
| Copy the data out of Burro in bulk | Get it from its publisher, under the publisher's licence. The sources page says where |
| Say or suggest that Burro backs you, your business or your listing | It does not |

### If you let, sell or manage homes, or find homes or tenants for others

This part is for anyone who uses Burro in the course of such a business: an estate agent, a letting agent, a landlord, a developer, a relocation agent, or anyone who acts for one.

You must not use anything Burro shows, or the order it puts areas in:

| You must not | For example |
|---|---|
| Choose which areas or homes to show, offer or recommend to a person, or which to leave out, because of that person's race, religion or belief, sex, disability, sexual orientation, gender reassignment, pregnancy or maternity, marriage or civil partnership, or age | Showing a family only the areas where the census counted many people of their ethnic group |
| Do the same because of who you think a person is, or who they are with | Guessing at a religion from a name |
| Tell or suggest to anyone that an area is for people like them, or is not | "You would feel more at home in" |
| Describe an area, a street or a home by who lives there, in a listing, an advert or a conversation | Quoting a figure for religion or ethnic group in a listing |
| Decide who to let to or sell to, or whose offer to take, by who lives in the area or by who the person is | |
| Set a rent, a price, a deposit or any other term by who lives in an area | |
| Ask Burro for more residents of an age, or more households of a kind, on behalf of a person who did not ask for it | Searching for areas with many older residents because a customer is retired |

By using Burro in the course of such a business you state to Burro that you will use it in none of these ways, and that what you do with it does not contravene the Equality Act 2010. `[SOLICITOR]`

You are responsible for your own duties under that Act. Burro is not advice about them. If Burro has reason to think that someone is using it in one of these ways, it may refuse to serve them. `[NOT BUILT: Burro has no way to tell one visitor from another, so it has no way to refuse one]`

`[FOUNDER: this part was written from sections 13, 29, 33, 111 and 112 of the Equality Act 2010, as printed at legislation.gov.uk and read twice on 24 September 2026. The equality regulator's own guidance was not read. The list of protected characteristics is section 4 of the Act. The statement in the last paragraph but one is there because section 112(2) gives a defence to a person who "relies on a statement" that an act is lawful, where that is reasonable. Whether a term that nobody signs is such a statement is not settled. The term forbids more than the Act does, on purpose: as read, the part of the Act on letting and selling does not apply to age or to marriage and civil partnership, and the term makes no such exception. The reading of the law is in residents-crime-and-equality.md, sections 7 to 9.]`

## 9. If something goes wrong

In plain words:

| | |
|---|---|
| Burro is free, and comes as it is | It may be wrong, out of date, slow or not there. Burro does not promise that it will be available, or that it will stay free |
| What Burro is responsible for | Loss or damage that is a foreseeable result of Burro failing to use reasonable care and skill. Loss is foreseeable if it is obvious that it would happen |
| What Burro is not responsible for | Loss that was not foreseeable. Loss that comes from using Burro in a way these terms say not to. Loss that comes from relying on made-up data: section 4 |
| A decision about a home is yours | Burro gives information and not advice. Do not rely on it alone to rent or buy a home |
| What these terms never limit | Liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence. Liability for fraud. Any right you have as a consumer that the law says cannot be taken away |
| If you use Burro for a business | Burro is not liable to you for loss of profit, loss of business or loss of opportunity |

`[FOUNDER: this section limits little on purpose. README.md, risk 11, says why, and what the other choice is. "Loss is foreseeable if it is obvious that it would happen" is wording common in terms written for consumers. It was not read at a source, and it is not the whole of what lawyers mean by the word.]`
