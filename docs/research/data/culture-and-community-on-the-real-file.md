# Culture, places of worship and centres: what the real file gave

Written 2026-09-24. A dated snapshot, not a source of truth.

The founder stated the period of the file of places on 2026-09-24: it is as at its publisher's release of 2026-09-23, and many records are years older. So the part round London has its receipt, and every figure here was worked out from it, through that receipt. It follows [culture nearby](culture-venues.md) and [places of worship and centres nearby](community-places.md), which were written before any real file was read. Where they differ from this page, this page stands.

**No figure here is of a named neighbourhood, and no name of a place was read.** The part holds no name. A figure is of the part, of London as a whole, or of a borough. No person has looked at a sample of the records.

Credit: Overture Maps Foundation, overturemaps.org. Records from Meta, Microsoft, PinMeTo, DAC and RenderSEO are under CDLA-Permissive-2.0, from Foursquare under Apache 2.0, and from AllThePlaces under CC0.

## 2. The tables of kinds, held to the file

Each table was read from an extraction of the publisher's table of categories. Each has now been held to the part, and names every category of its branch that the part holds. A category of the branch that is on no table stops the build.

| Table | Its branch | Categories the part holds | Counted | Left out by name before | Had not been met |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `derive/culture_kinds.py` | `arts_and_entertainment`, and `library` | 84 | 25 | 11, and one parent | 47 |
| `derive/worship_kinds.py` | `cultural_and_historic` | 24 | 13 | 1 | 10 |
| `derive/centres_nearby.py` | `community_and_government` | 48 | 1 | 2 | 45 |

- **No category that is counted was missing from a table.** So no count was short for a fault of a table.
- **What had not been met was passed by in silence.** A casino, a stadium, a dance club, a historic site, a town hall and a charity were each read as nothing of the measure, and nothing said so. Each is now left out by its name, with why.
- **A scout hall has a category of its own.** The part holds 1,702 records under `scout_hall` and 1,532 under `community_center`. A scout hall is not counted. How many are still filed as a community centre cannot be asked of a file that holds no name.
- **Of the branches of religion beside a place of worship, the part holds one.** 1,441 records are filed as `religious_organization`. No shrine, monastery, convent, seminary, retreat, ashram or Zen centre has a category of its own in the part. So whether they are counted changes no figure of this release.
- **The part tells apart five denominations of church, and no branch of any other kind.** Every mosque, synagogue, temple and gurdwara is filed under the head of its kind.
- **On the tables and in no record of the part:** four categories of museum, five that culture leaves out, 42 that are counted as a place of worship, and 17 of religion that are left out.
- **The rule that leaves out a stage school does nothing.** It asks what else a record says it is, and the file says that of 2,010 records in 683,409. No record of a kind says that it teaches. So a stage school that is filed as a theatre is counted as one.

## 11. What was not done

- No name was read, so no record was looked at to see whether it is what its category says.
- No figure was held against a register.
- No measure of this page was added to the measures of a build, and no release carries one.
- Core, the API and the website were not changed.
- The list still takes `sources` whole. The file's layout names the column `sources.list.element.dataset`, and to take it alone is another part and another fetch.
- Nothing was done about a figure that moves between releases. One release is pinned.

## 12. Counted a second time

Every figure of sections 1 to 5 was worked out a second time, by code that shares nothing with the modules named above. It opened each file through its receipt, as a step does. It decided a kind from the path of categories of a record and not from a table of names, measured a distance as a geodesic, and held a record to the outlines of MSOAs and not of LSOAs. That code is not committed.

| What | What the second count found |
|---|---|
| Rows in the part, in the box and inside London | The same: 683,409, 498,788 and 432,481 |
| Records and buildings of each kind, in the part and standing in London | The same, of all 14 kinds |
| Buildings standing in each borough, of each kind | The same in every borough |
| Output areas left out at the edge, and areas with no figure | The same: 311, and 10 |
| Venues within 800 m of home | The same in 983 areas of 992. In 9 it differs by 0.1 |
| Venues for each 1,000 homes | The same in 990 areas. In 2 it differs by 0.1 |
| Kinds within 800 m | The same in every area |
| Places of worship, each of the six kinds, and the two centres | The same in every area for five of the nine figures. Churches differ by 0.1 in 8 areas, places of worship in 4, and mosques, Hindu temples and community centres in 1 each |
| Rank correlations with the distance from the middle, and with a count of every record | The same to two decimal places, for all 12 figures |
| Rank correlations with homes per hectare | Lower by 0.05 at most. The second count took homes as the households of the census over the whole outline of an MSOA. The measure of homes takes them from the council tax lists |
| Going out on 65 in 100 | The same bands in every borough, with food and town centres as the two other branches worked them out |

Where a figure differs it is by one in the last place. A building that stands at the very end of the reach is within it by one way of measuring a distance and beyond it by another.

The second count holds the figures to the file. It does not hold the file to London: a record that is filed under the wrong kind, or a building the file does not hold, is counted the same way twice.
