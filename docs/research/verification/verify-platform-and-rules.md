# verify:platform-and-rules - Platform vendors, Apple rules and UK regulation

I checked 17 claims against primary sources on 2026-09-23. Thirteen hold, three are partly wrong or out of date, and one was not verified. Nothing found makes the plan illegal or forces a change of vendor, but three corrections change what gets built or budgeted.

The three corrections:
1. Analytics. The two reports disagree. The compliance report assumes aggregate, cookieless analytics with no consent banner. The platform report picks PostHog with funnels, flags and session recordings. ICO guidance says the statistical-purposes exception does not cover tracking individual visitors or recordings of them. One of the two has to give.
2. Vercel Pro allowances. Since 8 Sep 2026 new Pro teams get Flat Rate CDN with 1M requests and 1 TB, not 10M requests. The next tier is USD 20 per month for 10M.
3. MapLibre Native iOS PMTiles caching. The report says PMTiles sources are not cached. An ambient cache shipped in 6.27.0 (June 2026). The official docs page is stale. The decision to serve z/x/y tiles from a Worker still stands, because maintainers call PMTiles on native "still a bit experimental".

One claim was upgraded: the DMCC subscription regime date of January 2027, which the report had only from law firms, is confirmed by the Prime Minister's Office press release of 9 Aug 2026.

Limits of this check. No web search was made: everything came from pages opened at known addresses. Apple guidelines, npm, PyPI and GitHub files were read raw. GOV.UK, ICO, Ofcom, Supabase, Fly, Vercel and Cloudflare pages were read through a reader that summarises, so quotes from those may differ from the page by a word. What was read of Fly's per-region price table did not agree from one reading to the next. The EHRC code PDF was not read, so the estate-agent passage remains unverified. No repository files were touched.

## Checks
- [confirmed] Apple guidelines 4.8, 5.1.1(v) and 4.2: offering Google sign-in requires an equivalent privacy-preserving login; apps without significant account features must work without login; account creation requires in-app deletion; the app must be more than a repackaged website.
- [confirmed] Apple guideline 5.1.2(i) requires clear disclosure and explicit permission before personal data is shared with third-party AI.
- [confirmed] Apple guidelines 3.1.1, 3.1.1(a) and 3.1.3(b): premium features unlocked in the app must use In-App Purchase; outside entitled storefronts and the US, no links or calls to action to other purchase methods; web-bought subscriptions may be honoured if also sold as IAP. The UK is not an entitled storefront.
- [confirmed] The CMA steering requirement for Apple in the UK is proposed, not decided: consultation opened 30 Jun 2026, closed 28 Jul, responses published 14 Aug, no decision or timetable.
- [confirmed] The DMCC Act subscription contracts regime commences in January 2027, brought forward from spring 2027.
- [confirmed] PECR now exempts storage and access for statistical purposes from consent, subject to clear information, a simple way to object and no third-party own use; ICO guidance was finalised 29 Apr 2026.
- [partly_wrong] [impact major] (platform-stack and accounts-compliance (the two conflict)) Product analytics can run with no consent banner. The compliance report bases this on cookieless aggregate analytics; the platform report selects PostHog Cloud EU for events, funnels, feature flags and session recordings.
  - evidence: The same ICO exceptions chapter says the statistical purposes exception 'does not allow you to monitor or track individual visitors', excludes 'tracking or profiling individual visitors or categories of visitors', and lists 'logs or recordings of individual visitors to your website and the actions they took' as not covered. Feature flags and A/B assignment are not statistical purposes. PostHog's own GDPR page says it 'may be considered a data processor' and that EU Cloud is hosted in Frankfurt, which fits the processor condition, but person-level funnels and session replay fall outside the exception. The guidance does list 'preventing or detecting technical faults' under strictly necessary, which supports Sentry error reporting without consent, but not replay.
  - correction: Pick one path before building. Either aggregate-only analytics (Plausible, or PostHog configured with no person profiles, no persistent identifiers and no session replay) with a notice and opt-out and no banner, or full PostHog behind a consent prompt on both web and iOS. Feature flags should be evaluated server side from the entitlements table rather than a client SDK that stores an identifier. Record the choice in the DPIA.
- [confirmed] ICO fee is GBP 52 for tier 1 (GBP 47 by direct debit), and since 19 Jun 2026 controllers must run a data protection complaints process with acknowledgement within 30 days.
- [confirmed] Ofcom's position: a chatbot that only lets people interact with the chatbot and does not search multiple websites or databases is outside the Online Safety Act; section 229 excludes a service that searches just one website or database.
- [could_not_verify] [impact none] (accounts-compliance (passage listed there as unverified)) The EHRC Services Code came into force on 5 Aug 2026 and treats an estate agent providing information about properties as a service to the public under Part 3 of the Equality Act.
  - evidence: Confirmed: the GOV.UK publication page (published 21 May 2026) states the code came into force on 5 August 2026 and the 2011 code was revoked the same day. Confirmed: Equality Act 2010 s.29(1), 'A person (a service-provider) concerned with the provision of a service to the public or a section of the public (for payment or not) must not discriminate against a person requiring the service'. The code's paragraphs 3.6 to 3.7 as returned say the obligation 'also applies to the provision of services on a website'. Not confirmed: the estate-agent passage. The HTML read found no mention of 'estate agent', but the page is long and may not have been read to its end. The PDF was not read. Reading the PDF and searching for 'estate agent' would settle it.
  - correction: Rest the fairness policy on s.29 and the code's website paragraph, which are confirmed. Do not quote the estate-agent example in policy documents until someone has read it in the code.
- [confirmed] Supabase Pro at USD 25 per month in London (eu-west-2) provides Postgres with PostGIS and pgvector, 8 GB disk, 100k MAU auth and native Sign in with Apple through the Swift SDK; no h3 extension.
- [confirmed] Fly.io has a London region (lhr) and a shared-cpu-1x 1 GB machine costs USD 6.46 per month; AWS App Runner closed to new customers on 2026-04-30.
- [partly_wrong] [impact minor] (platform-stack) Vercel Hobby is non-commercial so Pro is required at launch; Pro is USD 20 per seat per month and includes 1 TB transfer and 10M edge requests.

'If your total usage for a billing cycle exceeds your monthly capacity, Vercel moves you to a higher tier at the start of the next cycle. A 
  - correction: Budget Vercel at USD 20 to 40 per month, not a flat 20. One million requests is roughly 20,000 to 50,000 page views for a Next.js app, which a public launch could pass. - [confirmed] swift-openapi-generator 1.13.1 supports OpenAPI 3.0 and 3.1 but ignores security schemes and validation keywords; openapi-typescript 7.13.0 has a peer dependency on TypeScript ^5 while TypeScript latest is 7.0.2.

## New facts
- Supabase direct database connections are IPv6 by default ('Direct connections are on IPv6, or on IPv4 if the project has the IPv4 add-on'). The Supavisor pooler 'Always uses an IPv4 address'. Transaction mode 'does not support prepared statements'. Fly docs say Machines 'have IPv6 addresses from which they make requests to the wider internet', so a direct connection from Fly should work, but this was not tested.
- Supabase's bundled PostGIS is 3.3.7 and pgvector is 0.8.0 (supabase/postgres README). Neon was reported at PostGIS 3.5. Any pipeline SQL that relies on PostGIS 3.4 or 3.5 functions will not run on Supabase.
- Fly.io machine prices vary by region through a per-region markup. The report's cost line assumed a single price.
- Vercel changed Pro billing on 8 Sep 2026 (Flat Rate CDN, on by default for new Pro teams). Spend management notifications default to USD 200 per billing cycle.
- Anthropic's usage policy lists housing eligibility decisions among high-risk use cases that need review by a qualified professional and disclosure of AI use. Recommending neighbourhoods is not an eligibility decision, but features such as 'will I be approved' or affordability verdicts would move toward it. The policy also requires consumer-facing chatbots to disclose AI 'at a minimum at the beginning of each chat session'. Effective date shown: 15 September 2025. Wording came through a reader that summarises and should be read directly before it is quoted.
- Anthropic first-party API data residency is confirmed from the raw docs page: inference_geo accepts only 'global' or 'us', workspace geo is 'us' only, and US-only inference costs 1.1x. There is no UK or EU option. Retention is confirmed as deletion 'within 30 days', with flagged content kept 'up to 2 years' and classifier scores 'up to 7 years'.
- The 9 Aug 2026 press release also announces an autumn 2026 consultation on banning fake 'was' prices, invented discounts and misleading RRPs under the DMCC Act. This will matter for premium pricing and launch-discount pages.
- ICO guidance places 'preventing or detecting technical faults' and 'authenticating the subscriber or user' within the strictly necessary exception. That supports error reporting and auth session storage without consent on both web and iOS.
- Apple added a requirement dated 9 Sep 2026 that iOS and iPadOS uploads must target iOS 13 or later. No effect on Burro, which targets iOS 17 or 18. The Xcode 26 and iOS 26 SDK requirement since 28 Apr 2026 is confirmed. No Xcode 27 deadline is listed yet.
- Xcode Cloud's 25 free compute hours per month and RevenueCat's free tier up to USD 2,500 monthly tracked revenue then 1% are both confirmed on the vendors' pages. App Store Small Business Program is 15% with a USD 1 million threshold and requires enrolment.
- The two reports disagree on the iOS map: the compliance report lists MapKit as evidence of native value for guideline 4.2, while the platform report rejects MapKit for MapLibre Native. The 4.2 case should rest on saved shortlists, share sheet, offline shortlist and native navigation instead.
- Supabase's signing-keys doc confirms ES256 and RS256 support and the JWKS URL format https://project-id.supabase.co/auth/v1/.well-known/jwks.json, cached at the edge for 10 minutes. Whether a new project uses asymmetric keys by default was not reliably readable and must be checked in the dashboard.
