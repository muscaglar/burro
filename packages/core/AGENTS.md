# packages/core

Burro's ranking engine: the catalogue of vibes, the preference spec, the reducer, `rank()`, likeness, the portrait, facts, the verifier and the rule-based interpreter. It is built to [docs/design/contract.md](../../docs/design/contract.md); if the two disagree, change one of them in the same commit.

- Pure Python, no IO. Nothing here opens a file, reads the clock, the environment or a random source, or logs. `tests/test_purity.py` holds the list of imports that are allowed. The only dependency is pydantic.
- `FeatureId` and `TagId` in `ids.py` are the allowlist of ADR 0006. Adding a feature means an enum member, a `FEATURES` row and a row in the contract, in one change. Nothing that describes who lives somewhere gets an id, and `Describes` has no member for it.
- A vibe is a `Tag`: a recipe over features. `checked_recipe()` holds every recipe to its rules when the catalogue is imported, so a recipe that breaks one cannot be served. Gritty is one vibe: a scale from Polished to Gritty, and the one recipe that holds recorded crime (`HOLDS_CRIME`, ADR 0013 as amended). Works and warehouses is a part of it, and `tags_of()` serves no vibe of that name where Gritty is carried. A release says `b` in `manifest.gritty_variant` where it carries Gritty, as the committed release and a build of London do, and `a` where it holds no recorded crime: it then carries Works and warehouses in Gritty's place. Do not let a second recipe hold recorded crime, and do not change Gritty's: each is changing ADR 0006 or 0013.
- A count that is shown and never ranked on stands in no recipe and takes no weight. `RANKED_AS` names the rate that is ranked on in its place: places to eat and drink, and cultural venues, each for every 1,000 homes. `checked_recipe()` refuses a recipe that holds such a count.
- A distance that is a straight line says so in its name, and is in metres. Do not name one a walk.
- A vibe is said as a band, one of five, and never as a percentage, a score or a rank. `band_of()` is the only place a band is worked out, in whole areas. The mid-rank percentile is for ranking alone.
- In an explanation a vibe is said in short: where the area sits, and what that rests on where it is part of a recipe. `IN_SHORT` holds how, and each is the start of the vibe's full statement, word for word. The dates and the line that the recipe is a judgement stay in the fact, for a client to show with the source. Do not take either out of the fact.
- A result shows a vibe nobody asked for only where the area has more of it than most, or sits towards an end of a scale: a vibe that runs one way is never shown at its least unless it was asked for. A vibe whose recipe holds recorded crime is on a result only where it was asked for.
- Likeness is counted on measured features and never on a vibe, a nuisance, recorded crime, cost or a journey. `may_be_compared()` refuses a nuisance whatever a feature's own flag says. The areas most alike come first by the count their sentence gives, so that the order can be checked on the page.
- Any change to the arithmetic in `rank.py`, to a rule of the reducer, or to a rule of what an explanation says, bumps `ENGINE_VERSION`. Any change to `FEATURES` or `TAGS` bumps `CATALOGUE_VERSION`. The worked example in `tests/test_rank.py` is the contract's, to four decimals: if it fails, the arithmetic moved.
- A spec changes only through `reducer.apply`. Records are frozen; copy one with `.replace()`, which validates, never with `model_copy(update=...)`, which does not.
- What a person says outweighs what they did not say, and the reducer is where that happens: a mention is worth a half, and the defaults give way to a quarter, rounded down to a step, once a wish is applied, so that one thing said outweighs all that was not. What is said of the place leads: a journey weighs 0.40 and a budget 0.30 until a person moves them, which is less than one mention each, and a firm limit is a filter and no weight. It is written into the spec. `rank()` knows nothing of it and stays a function of the spec and the release.
- What a person takes off stays off. A feature that some tenure weighs by default leaves an entry of 0 behind when it is taken off, which `canonical()`, `check_spec()` and `rank()` pass over. Do not tidy it away: it is what stops a change of tenure bringing the default back.
- **The rule-based reader applies a prompt only when the whole of it is plain.** What is plain is the grammar in `grammar.py`, made of the words in `vocabulary.py` and the things in `lexicon.py`. One token the grammar does not place makes the whole prompt not plain, and then nothing is applied, not even a budget: the reader offers what it noticed as suggestions, with the direction left to the person, and says what it made nothing of. It never guesses a direction. There is no list of the words that turn a wish round, because no such list is ever complete, and no single word is ever the fault: "pubs are so noisy" is made of words that are each harmless.
- If a wish is read backwards, the grammar makes a prompt it should not: tighten the rule that made it, and add the sentence to `tests/sentences.py`. If a plain wish is not applied, a word or a form is missing: add it only where the grammar can place it and it can turn no wish round there, say why in its group, and move the count in `tests/test_vocabulary.py`. Never add a past tense, a third person or a word that asks. The grammar is wide for a budget, a tenure, the size of a home and a journey, which are made of numbers, names and closed lists. It is narrow for a wish about character: no word calls up a vibe but a phrase of the lexicon, and a new one is a case in `evals/` first.
- A sentence ends at a full stop, `?`, `!` or a line break, and nowhere else. A token is never split at a mark inside it, and a name is never put together across a mark, a hyphen or a line break. A comma and a semicolon join two items, and any other mark between two tokens makes the prompt not plain. A question is not plain.
- Every edit the reader makes says which words it rests on, as offsets in `InterpretResult.rests_on`. A suggestion says where its words stand, and `unread` where the words stand that nothing was made of. `unmet` holds `other` exactly when `unread` is not empty. Nothing in core keeps the words.
- A word for a smart area, "affluent", "posh", is read as of the place and is only ever offered, in two ways for the person to choose from: towards Polished, and what homes sell for, dearer. `price_median` is a figure of what was paid for homes and never of who lives somewhere: it stands in no vibe and no likeness, and no word applies it. A word for character, "identity", "soulless", is offered three ways, whatever is said of it. Neither is ever applied, and nothing about who lives somewhere is read into either: beside a word for people, "posh locals", "a Polish character", each is a request about them and is offered nothing. `Target.one_way` and `Target.whatever` in `lexicon.py` hold the two rules, and `_WHO` and `OF_A_PEOPLE` the words.
- Words that say what the search already holds are heard: they are offered nowhere, because to choose them would change nothing, and they are not reported as unread. Words whose every edit would be turned away are unread.
- `tests/test_vocabulary.py` holds the reader to this three ways: the adversary's sentences in `tests/sentences.py`, every thing and every name beside 600 words it does not know, and the share of plain wishes it still applies, which has a floor. The generated test draws a fixed sample in `make ci` and runs in full with `make test ARGS="-m full"`. `WORDS_OF_DOUBT` is not read by the reader: it is there for the test that no plain word is one, and for the model-backed reader.
- What is said after a thing never begins a new wish. "Nearby", "on my doorstep" and "would be good" may be said of every thing of a list, so after a turn they leave the list a list: joined by "or" the turn carries, and joined by a comma or "and" the prompt is not plain. Only what stands before a thing, a turn of its own, "but", or the speaker's own wish begins a new one, and after "or" only a turn of its own does.
- A wish that was turned round, "no parks", "not near the shops", never raises a weight. Only a feature in `NUISANCES` is cared about more for being wanted less, and a nuisance that is only named, or that the person says they like, makes no edit: "I like noise". An end of a scale that is turned away is a wish for the other end: "not buzzy" is Pace towards Calm.
- The reader takes a name only when the whole of it was typed. Words after "work at" and "30 minutes to" that are the whole of no name are asked about and add no journey. Part of a word is offered by a search and never taken. The question is asked in a prompt that is not plain too, where the grammar makes the sentence it stands in: it is the one edit such a prompt may hold, and it carries no place.
- A range of minutes is read as the longer of the two, and is a firm limit there. The edit says its minutes were assumed. A range of anything else is never read.
- What makes a limit firm is two lists in `vocabulary.py`, one for each kind of limit: `FIRM_OF_MONEY` and `FIRM_OF_MINUTES`. "Max" and "up to" make a budget firm, and "max" and "within" a number of minutes. "Up to" makes no journey firm, and "under", "around" and "maximum" make nothing firm. A budget or a journey that is only noticed is offered as a firm limit where those words stand against its number, and rests on them. Each list is a call made for the founder: change one only with them.
- A number typed with an "m" and no pound sign, "5m", "1.5m", is as likely minutes, metres or miles as millions. A word that caps, "within", "max", is no word of money. It is a budget only where the sentence says what it is for, and is offered or unread anywhere else. `Grammar._an_m_is_money` holds the rule.
- A place that is commuted from, "I commute from", may be where the person lives now. With no time said of it the journey is offered, and never applied.
- Missing data is never filled in. A value that is not known is `None`, and zero never stands for unknown, except as a sentinel inside `Operations`.
- An error message holds paths, rows and codes, and never a value: not a place id, not a name, not what a person typed. `InterpretResult` and `ExplainInput` have no field that can hold user text.
- A rule of `parse_release` is one small function listed in `RULES`. A new rule needs a row in `BROKEN` in `tests/test_release.py` that shows it refusing a release.
- A release says whether it is a preview, which is a release that is not finished. Only a preview may leave the sources of its journeys or its stations unstated, and only where it holds none. Do not widen that to another file: a figure with no source is no figure. See ADR 0017.
- A release that holds no journey's end, no place to reach, no cost or no station is a preview, and one that says otherwise is refused: `finished_release_is_whole`. A release is not finished on its own word.
- A release that is not made up is served only with what it was built with. `open_served` holds the manifest, the evidence and the lock to the hashes the build wrote. Core reads none of the evidence, and still opens no file: the caller hands it the bytes. Do not let a release that is not made up through `open_served` with nothing beside it. See ADR 0018.
- A figure is held to what its unit can be: a percentage from 0 to 100, any other unit not below nought. An area is held to the rest of its own release, and to no place on the map: a test draws its town in open sea under real ids, and a box round London would refuse it.
- Every template must pass the verifier, and every fact kind must have a template that is true of it.
- A comparison in a sentence is literally true of the release: the share of areas strictly beyond, rounded down, with the areas that are level counted and said. It is said from the side of its role: a reason from the better side, a trade-off from the worse. The mid-rank percentile is for scoring and is never printed in a sentence.
- The verifier is enough for fixed text and not for a model. `explain()` takes `TemplateExplainer` and refuses any other explainer, in code. Do not loosen that check until the verifier is extended as the contract's section 11 says, and do both in one change. Two tests marked as expected to fail hold the sentences the verifier lets through. A test that plants a sentence does it from a subclass of `TemplateExplainer`.
- A reason never states a shortfall: a home over budget and a journey over its cap are never given as a reason, whatever they add to the score. And a trade-off is never something the area does well: it is a component worth less than `TRADE_OFF_MAX_UTILITY`, or a shortfall. It is worse than most and bad in itself: a walk, a time or a distance at or under its figure in `NEVER_A_TRADE_OFF` is never one, however many areas are closer. That table is in the catalogue, and a test holds it to every feature in minutes or metres. What lies between the two thresholds is in no sentence. An area that does nothing badly has no trade-off.
- The journeys are scored on the legs that have a time. A leg with no time is said to be missing, and never lifts an area that is known to be far from somewhere else.
- An area with no figure for a thing that was asked for stands below every area that has one. It is ranked, and its fit is worked out on the rest as before. `asked_for()` in `rank.py` says what was asked: everything but a usual setting, which is a feature the tenure weighs by default that stands at the weight nobody chose, whole or given way. It is read from the weight and never from a provenance. A journey, a budget and a vibe are always asked for.
- The character that counts is covered on its own. An area with a figure for under half of the features and vibes of a search, by weight, is not ranked, whatever is known of its journeys and its cost: it is `unranked` with `character_unknown`, and says what it lacks. What counts as character never depends on a provenance, because two specs with one canonical form rank the same.
- **The census is apart.** `census.py` holds the figures an area's page shows of who lived there, and nothing that ranks, explains, compares or reads a sentence can be handed one: `Release` returns none, no other module imports it, and it is told of a release only its id, whether it is made up, and its areas. Do not import it here, do not export it from `burro_core`, and give no `FeatureId`, `TagId` or `FactKind` the name of what is shown and never ranked on: ethnic group, religion, country of birth. A measure of age or of households that feeds a vibe is a feature of the catalogue, read from the release, and never from a census. `tests/test_census.py` holds each of these.
- What is served of a census figure is worked out in `panel()` and nowhere else: a share in whole per cents, its count, and the whole city's share beside it. A share under 1 in 100 is said in words, with no count. The rows keep the order of the file. No word of `CENSUS_2021` or `MADE_UP` says what a figure means: `NEVER_SAID` is the list, and a test reads every word against it.
- Tests run offline and share their releases through `tests/support.py`, imported as `from .support import ...`. Every name in it is made up, and was looked up in an encyclopaedia on 2026-09-23. A name that was found to be real is held in `packages/pipeline/tests/test_synthetic_release.py`, where a test looks for it in every file, the name of a constant included. `tests/test_fixture.py` reads the committed synthetic release, for what must hold of the release that is served. The test reads the bytes; core still opens no file.

## Keeping the contract true

What the code does beyond the first draft of the contract has been written into it: `Release.cutoff`, `InMemoryRelease.documents()`, `Fact.template`, the `station_nearby` template, the names of the refusals that are not one of the sixteen rules, the clauses a comparison is stated in, and what the rule interpreter does with a plain mention, with a word that turns, with a word the grammar does not place and with a name that has an article. If you add something the contract does not say, write it in where it applies, in the same change.
