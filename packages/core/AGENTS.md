# packages/core

Burro's ranking engine: the catalogue of vibes, the preference spec, the reducer, `rank()`, likeness, the portrait, facts, the verifier and the rule-based interpreter. It is built to [docs/design/contract.md](../../docs/design/contract.md); if the two disagree, change one of them in the same commit.

- Pure Python, no IO. Nothing here opens a file, reads the clock, the environment or a random source, or logs. `tests/test_purity.py` holds the list of imports that are allowed. The only dependency is pydantic.
- `FeatureId` and `TagId` in `ids.py` are the allowlist of ADR 0006. Adding a feature means an enum member, a `FEATURES` row and a row in the contract, in one change. Four features describe who lived somewhere, from the census of 2021: residents of two ages and households of two kinds (`COUNTS_RESIDENTS`). Nothing else that describes who lives somewhere gets an id, and a test holds the list to those four.
- **What counts who lives somewhere is held to six rules, and they do not bend** (ADR 0006, as amended). It is read high and never low: a person may ask for more of it and never for fewer, in an edit, a spec, a recipe or any words. It stands in a vibe with one way and never in a scale. It counts towards no likeness. Its label says who is counted and ends ", Census 2021", its unit is a share in 100, and a release holds no count of people. It is of age or of households and nothing else: no ethnic group, religion, country of birth, income, health or qualifications, in a name or in a word that is read. `checked_recipe()` holds a recipe that counts residents to five rules of its own: read high, one way, no part over 40 in 100, the census named in its meaning, and no `strip`, so that no result shows it unasked and no portrait leads with it. Two vibes hold one: Family area and Young professionals (`HOLDS_RESIDENTS`).
- A phrase for those who are counted is a phrase of the lexicon with a note, so it is offered and never applied, towards more and no other way: "young professionals", "families", "retirees". Beside a word that turns or takes off it is a wish for fewer of a group of people, and draws the notice and nothing else: `may_ask_for_fewer()` in `interpret.py`. A prompt that draws the notice is offered nothing that counts who lives somewhere. Every other word for people is in `POLICY_LEXICON`, and draws the notice.
- **A release may carry what a person adjusted at the panel, and nothing else that is not core's** (ADR 0029). Of a vibe: the hundredths of its parts, its name, the names of its ends, and what it cannot see after the first line. Of a measure: its label and its short label, unless it counts who lived somewhere. `what_a_vibe_breaks()` and `what_a_measure_breaks()` in `release.py` hold what a release carries to every rule core's own are held to, and `name_is_plain()` holds a name to the words no sentence may say. No release adds a part to a recipe, takes one out or turns one round. `TAGS` and `FEATURES` are what a build starts from: read a name or a recipe from the release where one is at hand, and from core's own only where none is. Do not widen what may differ without a decision record.
- **A release and the file of changes it was built with can each be written by hand.** So what a person may adjust is held in `release.py`, where no hand can get round it, and never in the panel or the pipeline alone. `held_off_stays_held_off`: a vibe core places no area on is placed by no change to its shares. `names_name_no_place`: a name a person gave holds no name of a place the release holds. `changes_are_named` and `changes_are_locked`: a release that carries a change says which file made it so, and is served only beside a lock that names that file. A rule that guards what a person may change belongs here.
- A vibe is a `Tag`: a recipe over features. `checked_recipe()` holds every recipe to its rules when the catalogue is imported, so a recipe that breaks one cannot be served. Gritty is one vibe: a scale from Polished to Gritty, and the one recipe that holds recorded crime (`HOLDS_CRIME`, ADR 0013 as amended). Works and warehouses is a part of it, and `tags_of()` serves no vibe of that name where Gritty is carried. A release says `b` in `manifest.gritty_variant` where it carries Gritty, as the committed release and a build of London do, and `a` where it holds no recorded crime: it then carries Works and warehouses in Gritty's place. Do not let a second recipe hold recorded crime, and do not change Gritty's: each is changing ADR 0006 or 0013. Village feel is a rough guide (`Tag.sureness`, decided on 2026-09-25): it did not reach the bar the founder set, and is served all the same. It says so wherever it is shown, in the label and the sentence core holds (`ROUGH_GUIDE`, `WHY_A_ROUGH_GUIDE`), and it is never taken without a press of its own: no word applies it, its own name among them (`is_a_rough_guide` in `lexicon.py`), it holds no `strip`, and no likeness counts the measure that was made for it. Change no share of its recipe but with the founder. No vibe is held off by name today: `PLACED_ONLY_WITH` is empty, `tag_raw()` still holds its rule, and a vibe is named there, or taken out, only in a change the founder has seen.
- The traffic near homes is a nuisance that a person may rank on: `road_traffic_nearby`, since catalogue version 15. It is the flow past the busiest count point within 500 m of home, which its name calls a counting point, and the distance is one line, `TRAFFIC_WITHIN_M`, which stands in the label. Its publisher counts main roads and a sample of the rest, so an area may have no figure, and no figure is never little traffic: in Quiet streets the part is left out and the rest weighed again, and asked for by itself the area stands below every area that has a figure. Quiet streets holds it at 20 in 100, which it took from main roads: both are of the roads near a home. It is in no other recipe. Do not add it to Gritty's, which is as it was decided, or to Village feel's, which is the founder's. A flow is printed whole and with its separator, by its unit, `MOTOR_VEHICLES_A_DAY`. In words it is asked for as a nuisance is: "no traffic", "less traffic", "no busy roads". A main road is asked of as the homes beside one and as the traffic near home, so that a release which ranks no area on main roads alone still answers. A quiet road is a quiet street, which is the vibe. "On" is no word of the grammar, so "not on a busy road" is offered and never applied: do not add it to the words that turn, where "not on a budget" would be read as a wish.
- A count that is shown and never ranked on stands in no recipe and takes no weight. `RANKED_AS` names what is ranked on in its place: places to eat and drink, cultural venues, cafes, gyms, and pubs and bars, each for every 1,000 homes, and the routes of buses in place of the stops. `checked_recipe()` refuses a recipe that holds such a count.
- One thing is estimated, and says so wherever it is shown: a journey by public transport, where a release holds no time for it (ADR 0027). `estimate.py` holds the whole of it, and each number of it once, by name. It is served as a band against the limit, `likely_within`, `borderline` or `likely_beyond`, and never in minutes. A time that a release holds takes its place. Do not estimate anything else, and do not serve the estimate as a number.
- **A measure is named for a person who has never seen Burro**, since catalogue version 16. What is measured stands first, and what qualifies it after: "Distance to the nearest station entrance, in a straight line", "Homes within 100 m of a main road, as a share of all homes". A name keeps every claim it makes, and a test holds each: that a distance is a straight line, that an entrance is a marked one, that a practice is placed by its postcode, that a figure is modelled, what a share is a share of, and that a tier is "as Burro sorts the chains". A median is "the middle price". A unit is written out: "per hectare". No name says that its figure is of now: a figure is of the period its release gives, which stands beside it as its date, so a rise in what was paid names "the latest year". Every name is a phrase the reader knows, so a name holds no word that is in `UNKNOWN_WORDS` unless `test_vocabulary.py` lists it. A step of a build that holds a name of its own holds core's, word for word, or a build leaves the measure out: change both in one change. Three names are left until the founder has decided what becomes of their measures, `park_facilities`, `centre_small` and `centre_compact`, and the name of transport noise and the four that say who lived somewhere are held word for word.
- A distance that is a straight line says so in its name, and is in metres. Do not name one a walk. What near means of a station is said apart from the name of its measure, in `NEAR_A_STATION` in `catalogue.py`: within 800 m in a straight line is about a 10 to 15 minute walk, as the founder decided on 2026-09-25. It states the figure `NEVER_A_TRADE_OFF` holds for a station, an offer of the measure says it, and it is said of no other distance.
- The chains of grocers, gyms and coffee are 48 features of dimension `brands` (ADR 0026). Core names the kinds, the tiers and the chains, in `KINDS_OF_CHAIN`, `TIERS` and `CHAINS`, and never says which chain is of which tier: that table is the founder's, and is data of the pipeline's. The mix, `brand_mix`, is what is ranked on. The count and the distance of each tier are shown beside it and never weighed (`SHOWN_BESIDE_THE_MIX`). A chain is weighed only where a person asks for it by name, and one way: nearer. None stands in a vibe or in likeness. A name that a person types is offered and never applied.
- A vibe is said as a band, one of five, and never as a percentage, a score or a rank. `band_of()` is the only place a band is worked out, in whole areas. The mid-rank percentile is for ranking alone.
- **Every word core writes for a person is written for one who has never seen Burro**, as [the guide to the words](../../docs/design/words.md) asks: in whole sentences that are joined, in everyday words. Four words of the design are said in none: a release, a recipe, a part of one, and "counted from". A sentence says "the areas Burro compared", "the measurements that go into this vibe" and "where the bands run from". `tests/test_the_words_of_the_design.py` reads every fixed word and every sentence of every fact. A sentence of a fact says what its fact holds and no more: where a better sentence would need a slot the fact does not hold, say it without, as the sentence of a budget held against a range says "this kind of home" and names none. Never write a figure or a reason into a template. Where a sentence holds two figures and a third that is counted from one of them, and the fact holds no word for which, the sentence points at neither: a journey by public transport over its limit says "This journey is 28 minutes over", and not "That is", which stood after the time of a missed service. What is said of the census and of who lives somewhere says what it said, in the order a person says it, and gives no reason of its own (ADR 0006, 0014, 0028).
- In an explanation a vibe is said in short: where the area sits, and what that rests on where it is part of a recipe. `IN_SHORT` holds how, and each is the start of the vibe's full statement, word for word. The dates and the line that the recipe is a judgement stay in the fact, for a client to show with the source. Do not take either out of the fact.
- A result shows a vibe nobody asked for only where the area has more of it than most, or sits towards an end of a scale: a vibe that runs one way is never shown at its least unless it was asked for. A vibe whose recipe holds recorded crime is on a result only where it was asked for, and so is a vibe that counts who lives somewhere.
- Likeness is counted on measured features and never on a vibe, a nuisance, recorded crime, cost or a journey. `may_be_compared()` refuses a nuisance whatever a feature's own flag says. The areas most alike come first by the count their sentence gives, so that the order can be checked on the page.
- Any change to the arithmetic in `rank.py`, to a rule of the reducer, or to a rule of what an explanation says, bumps `ENGINE_VERSION`. Any change to `FEATURES` or `TAGS` bumps `CATALOGUE_VERSION`. The worked example in `tests/test_rank.py` is the contract's, to four decimals: if it fails, the arithmetic moved.
- A spec changes only through `reducer.apply`. Records are frozen; copy one with `.replace()`, which validates, never with `model_copy(update=...)`, which does not.
- What a person says outweighs what they did not say, and the reducer is where that happens: a mention is worth a half, and the defaults give way to a quarter, rounded down to a step, once a wish is applied, so that one thing said outweighs all that was not. What is said of the place leads: a journey weighs 0.40 and a budget 0.30 until a person moves them, which is less than one mention each, and a firm limit is a filter and no weight. It is written into the spec. `rank()` knows nothing of it and stays a function of the spec and the release.
- No fact gives a difference of nothing. A cost that is the budget to the pound is said to be at it, by `budget_at` and its two like, and a journey that takes the minutes of its limit holds `verdict` and no `margin`. `test_a_difference_of_nothing.py` holds every sentence that gives a difference.
- A firm budget is held against a median with a margin, and against a range with none. `over_a_firm_budget()` in `rank.py` is the one rule, and `FIRM_BUDGET_MARGIN_PERCENT` the one place the margin is written: an area is left out only where its median is more than a quarter over the budget, because about half of the homes behind a median sold for less. It is counted in whole pounds. The margin is the founder's to change, and a change to it bumps `ENGINE_VERSION`. A cost with no range holds the sentence that says so in its slot `half_sold`, and the `budget_fit` fact of a median over the budget prints it.
- A rent may be of a wider place than the area: `CostEstimate.of`, with `rents`, how many were recorded there, and `since`. It is a range as its publisher gives it, and every area of the place holds the same row. A budget is held against its median and never against its upper quartile, by `held_on_the_median()` in `rank.py`, with the margin of a firm budget. Wherever it is said, the place it is of is said with it: `IS_OF` and `COST_OF` in `facts.py` are the words, and `RENT_CAUTION` is what the publisher advises, which a page says once. Do not print a word for how sure it is: the count is what is shown.
- A median may say how many sales it rests on: `CostEstimate.sales`, with `since`, the first month of those sales. What it rests on is then what the count makes it, by `confidence_of()`, and never `unstated`. A release holds no median of fewer than `FEWEST_SALES`: a row of a sale is of one home, and a figure of a few would say what one sold for.
- **A visit is a kind of search of its own, and holds no budget and no kind of home** (contract, sections 4 and 5.3, rule 16; decided on 2026-09-26). `Tenure.VISIT` is the third tenure. A visit holds `NO_BUDGET` and no other budget, which `PreferenceSpec` refuses on construction: no amount, a weight of nothing, and a segment that stands for nothing, because the record of a budget cannot leave one out and the shape of a spec is as it was. `_budget` and `_setting` in `reducer.py` turn away an edit that would give a visit a home, `segment_not_for_tenure`, or a budget, `not_in_release`, and leave the search as it was. A search that becomes a visit drops the budget and the home and keeps all else, and one that ceases to be a visit takes the usual budget of its new kind. `rank()` and `facts_for()` ask a release for no cost of a visit, so no area is left out or moved for what a home costs, no fact of a visit is a `cost` or a `budget_fit`, and a release that holds no cost serves a visit whole. What a visitor is taken to mind is `_VISITOR_WEIGHTS` in `spec.py`, a judgement that is the founder's to change: given way it comes to 0.35, so that the one place a visitor names, at 0.40, outweighs it. Do not add a seventh measure to it, or a second at 0.40 or more, without the founder. `tests/test_a_visit.py` holds each.
- What a person takes off stays off. A feature that some tenure weighs by default leaves an entry of 0 behind when it is taken off, which `canonical()`, `check_spec()` and `rank()` pass over. Do not tidy it away: it is what stops a change of tenure bringing the default back.
- **The rule-based reader applies a prompt only when the whole of it is plain.** What is plain is the grammar in `grammar.py`, made of the words in `vocabulary.py` and the things in `lexicon.py`. One token the grammar does not place makes the whole prompt not plain, and then nothing is applied, not even a budget: the reader offers what it noticed as suggestions, with the direction left to the person, and says what it made nothing of. It never guesses a direction. There is no list of the words that turn a wish round, because no such list is ever complete, and no single word is ever the fault: "pubs are so noisy" is made of words that are each harmless.
- **What is offered is marked by the API, by the reader's own reading of each sentence** (contract, section 8.2, "The way the words give of a wish"; 2026-09-27). The reader still never guesses a direction, and still applies nothing of a prompt that is not plain. For whoever marks the way, `by_sentence(request, listed=True)` and `known_in()` hold a sentence that names nothing to the words core lists: "Moving next month. Somewhere leafy." leaves the second sentence read. Lists in `vocabulary.py` are for that caller and are read by the reader through none of them, but for the first where it says what is unread: `ASIDES`, what is said of the words alone, "honestly", "I think", and `SAYS_HOW_LONG`, what a heading says of the words under it, "short version"; `CARRIES_A_TURN`, "nor" and "than", which are no second turn, `TURNS_NOTHING`, what holds a word that turns and says some or only, "a few", "nothing but", and `TURNS_WHERE_IT_STANDS_ALONE`, "pass", which turns only where it is all that stands between two marks; `HEADS_WHAT_IS_WANTED`, "must haves", "priorities", and `OPENS_A_HEADING`, "what", "things"; `CANNOT_BEAR`, "hate", "avoid"; and where what is said next begins, `LEADS_IN_A_WISH`, `LEADS_IN_WHAT_IS_SAID_NEXT` and `GIVES_A_REASON`, so that a word that turns leads up to the thing it is said of and no further. A prompt that holds an aside, a heading or a word that turns nothing is not plain. `known_in()` and `taken_back_in()` say which sentences the reader reads and which a sentence beside them takes back, of those it reads and of those it does not. **Add to the words that begin what is said next only a word that cannot stand between a turn and the thing it turns**: "or" carries a turn, "with" joins a thing to the one before it, and "as" and "since" are as often said of a time. Add to `ASIDES` nothing that turns, asks, compares, weakens or names anybody but the speaker: `tests/test_what_the_words_give.py` holds it. The sentences an offer says of a thing the words turn round, or that waits for a person, are core's: `NOT_WANTED`, `NOT_WANTED_AND_COUNTED`, `DOES_NOT_MATTER` and `NOT_SAID_TO_BE_WANTED` in `interpret.py`.
- **An offer says whether the words name what it counts, and whether it waits for a person**: `Suggestion.by_name` and `only_by_choice`. `names_it()` in `lexicon.py` is the one rule of the first: a phrase the lexicon holds as `stated`, and never the name of a scale. `only_by_choice()` in `catalogue.py` is the one rule of the second: what counts who lived somewhere waits whatever the words are, and recorded crime and what `OFFERED_AND_NEVER_APPLIED` names wait where the words do not name them. A measure goes on that list, or comes off it, by a decision that is written down, and no client names one.
- If a wish is read backwards, the grammar makes a prompt it should not: tighten the rule that made it, and add the sentence to `tests/sentences.py`. If a plain wish is not applied, a word or a form is missing: add it only where the grammar can place it and it can turn no wish round there, say why in its group, and move the count in `tests/test_vocabulary.py`. Never add a past tense, a third person or a word that asks. The grammar is wide for a budget, a tenure, the size of a home and a journey, which are made of numbers, names and closed lists. It is narrow for a wish about character: no word calls up a vibe but a phrase of the lexicon, and a new one is a case in `evals/` first.
- A sentence ends at a full stop, `?`, `!` or a line break, and nowhere else. A token is never split at a mark inside it, and a name is never put together across a mark, a hyphen or a line break. A comma and a semicolon join two items, and any other mark between two tokens makes the prompt not plain. A question is not plain.
- Every edit the reader makes says which words it rests on, as offsets in `InterpretResult.rests_on`. A suggestion says where its words stand, and `unread` where the words stand that nothing was made of and that may have asked for something. A stretch that holds nothing but words that ask for nothing, "I want to live somewhere", is kept in `asks_nothing` and is not said to be unread: `ASKS_NOTHING` in `grammar.py` is made of lists the grammar holds, and a word goes on it only if it cannot turn, compare, weaken or say how much. `unmet` holds `other` exactly when either is not empty. **What is said of the words alone asks for nothing where it is the whole of a part of its sentence** (mended on 2026-09-27): "somewhere cheap, I think" was answered as "somewhere cheap" is, but that "I think" was said to be unread. `_said_of_the_words_alone` in `interpret.py` holds it, by `ASIDES`. One that stands among other words is said with them, as it was, and so is one in quotes. It makes the prompt no more plain, `other` is unmet as it was, and whoever asks a model by what nothing was made of asks as they did. Nothing in core keeps the words.
- A word for a smart area, "affluent", "posh", is read as of the place and is only ever offered, in three ways for the person to choose from: the mix of brands, more premium, which comes first; towards Polished; what homes sell for, dearer, and more homes in the higher council tax bands. "Cheap and cheerful", "unpretentious" and "down to earth" are offered the other end of the mix and nothing else. `price_median` and `homes_higher_bands` are figures of the homes of a place and never of who lives somewhere: each stands in no vibe and no likeness, and no word applies it. A word for a place on the rise, "up and coming", "on the up", "rising", is offered how far what homes sold for has risen, `price_rise_5y` and `price_rise_10y`, with the note that a rise is of prices that were paid and promises nothing. The features a word for a smart area is offered stand one to a line in `_SELLS_FOR_MORE`, so that a reading can be added beside the others. A word for character, "identity", "soulless", is offered three ways, whatever is said of it. Its note names the ways that are offered and no other, so a release that places no area on Village feel is never said to count one: `no_identity()` in `lexicon.py` makes the note. Neither is ever applied, and nothing about who lives somewhere is read into either: beside a word for people, "posh locals", "a Polish character", each is a request about them and is offered nothing. `Target.one_way` and `Target.whatever` in `lexicon.py` hold the two rules, and `_WHO` and `OF_A_PEOPLE` the words.
- Words that say what the search already holds are heard: they are offered nowhere, because to choose them would change nothing, and they are not reported as unread. Words whose every edit would be turned away are unread.
- `tests/test_vocabulary.py` holds the reader to this three ways: the adversary's sentences in `tests/sentences.py`, every thing and every name beside 600 words it does not know, and the share of plain wishes it still applies, which has a floor. The generated test draws a fixed sample in `make ci` and runs in full with `make test ARGS="-m full"`. `WORDS_OF_DOUBT` is not read by the reader: it is there for the test that no plain word is one, and for the model-backed reader. So are `DREADS`, what a person dreads, thinks little of or cannot bear, `WHO_ELSE` and `WISHES_OF_ANOTHER`, which say that a wish is somebody else's, and `STANDS_FOR`: the guard on a model marks no guess where one stands about a thing. A word for one is added there, and never in the API. `ENDS_NAMED_AS_HOMES` in `lexicon.py` is for the guard too: a word for a home is no phrase of the lexicon, and names an end of Houses or flats only to whoever has been told that the scale is meant.
- What is said after a thing never begins a new wish. "Nearby", "on my doorstep" and "would be good" may be said of every thing of a list, so after a turn they leave the list a list: joined by "or" the turn carries, and joined by a comma or "and" the prompt is not plain. Only what stands before a thing, a turn of its own, "but", or the speaker's own wish begins a new one, and after "or" only a turn of its own does. **A wish with no speaker of its own begins nothing after "or", where a turn is carried** (mended on 2026-09-27): "I don't want pubs or need a station" asks for neither, was applied with the station raised, and is not plain. `Wish.unspoken` says that a wish has no speaker, and `_one_turn_one_thing` holds the rule.
- A wish that was turned round, "no parks", "not near the shops", never raises a weight. Only a feature in `NUISANCES` is cared about more for being wanted less, and a nuisance that is only named, or that the person says they like, makes no edit: "I like noise". An end of a scale that is turned away is a wish for the other end: "not buzzy" is Pace towards Calm.
- The reader takes a name only when the whole of it was typed. Words after "work at" and "30 minutes to" that are the whole of no name are asked about and add no journey. Part of a word is offered by a search and never taken. The question is asked in a prompt that is not plain too, where the grammar makes the sentence it stands in: it is the one edit such a prompt may hold, and it carries no place. It is asked where the grammar makes the part of a sentence it stands in as well, where the part is the whole of the journey and a place bears a part of the name: `asks_which_place` in `grammar.py` and `_asked_in_part` in `interpret.py`. "Leafy and quiet, 30 minutes to Pellam, honestly" dropped the journey, and offered a rule for the area that answers to the name. Nothing is asked of a sentence that asks, that the next takes back, or that holds a word for staying away. `tests/test_a_journey_to_part_of_a_name.py` holds it.
- A range of minutes is read as the longer of the two, and is a firm limit there. The edit says its minutes were assumed. A range of anything else is never read.
- **A time in hours is a number of minutes, and is read where a number is read.** "1 hour 15", "2 hours", "1.5 hours", "1h" and "an hour and a quarter" are each one number: `hours_at` in `reading.py`, and `Item.hours` says that it was so said. Whatever may be said of minutes may be said of it. After a number in figures the letter "h" is a word for hours, against the number or after a space, "1h30", "1 h 30": `HOUR_OR_ITS_LETTER`. Typed with a point it is read only as a half or a quarter of an hour, "1.5 hours", "1.25 hours", `_AFTER_THE_POINT`: "1.30 hours" is an hour and a half as a clock shows it and 78 minutes by its arithmetic, so it is no time the reader reads. No word of it is a word of the grammar, so "hours" with no number of them is a word the reader does not know, and a time is never put together across a mark. Minutes that follow a whole number of hours are read with them, side by side: with no word for minutes they are five or more, and no number of bedrooms. **A number that stands where the minutes of a longer time stand is never read by itself**: after a time in hours, whatever mark parts them, "1 hour, 15 minutes", straight after a word for hours, straight after another number that may be the hours, "1 15", and after a time in hours and a word that joins the two, where it is less than that time, "1 hour 15 or 20". `part_of_a_longer_time` in `grammar.py` makes its sentence not plain, and no journey is offered at the place it stands before. "1 hour 15 to" was offered as 15 minutes, and one press took it. **Nor is an hour that is part of what stands before it**: "a quarter of an hour" and "three quarters of an hour" are read whole, `_PARTS_OF_AN_HOUR` in `reading.py`, and any other time in hours straight after "of", "a third of an hour", "most of an hour", is read by itself only where a word for a journey or for the most stands before "of": `_A_LENGTH_OF` in `grammar.py`. "Three quarters of an hour to" was offered as 60 minutes. A range of hours is a range of something that is not minutes. Do not add a word for hours to the grammar: read the number.
- **A time that stands before a place and is not taken is never made up for by the usual one.** In a prompt that is not plain a journey with no time is offered, and is added with the usual 45 minutes. Where a time was typed where the time of a journey stands, "1 hour, 15 minutes to", "1.30 hours to", "a few hours to", "1h75 to", "24 hours to", and no minutes were taken from it, the place is offered with nothing to choose and the note `TIME_NOT_TAKEN`: one press added a journey of 45 minutes, which nobody said. `_time_stands_before` in `interpret.py` holds where a time stands, and `Item.figures` a word that holds a figure the reader did not read. A time in hours stands there with a word or two after it as well, "an hour or so to", "an hour n a half to", which `_SAID_OF_A_TIME` lists. The service drops a model's reading of a journey to such a place, as it does of one to a place to stay away from, and holds a number that is part of a longer time to be no number of minutes that was typed.
- **A time beside a place is the time of the journey to it, wherever it stands** (contract, section 8.2; mended on 2026-09-26, when the website came to take what is offered without asking). With a word or two between it and the place, "40 minutes or so to", "35 minutes on the tube to", after the place, "Cindermoor Works within 40 minutes", and apart from it, "Cindermoor Works, 40 minutes": each was offered at the usual 45 minutes, and the service said that the person gave none. `_times_of` reads every time of a sentence with what is said of it, and `_times_given` gives each to the journey the words give it to: the place it leads to or follows, the one journey of the prompt or of its sentence, and each journey where it is said with a word for one. `LEAVES_A_TIME_AS_IT_IS` and `THE_MOST_AFTER` in `vocabulary.py` and `ON_TRANSPORT` in `grammar.py` are what may be said of a time, and none is a word of the grammar: a plain prompt is applied as it was. **What makes it a limit is `FIRM_OF_MINUTES` against its own number, and no other list.** A time is never read from what is said of a thing or of a kind of thing, "45 minute classes", and one that is said of what the reader does not know is the time of no journey (`_said_before`). Where the reader cannot tell which journey a time is for, the journey is offered with no time and the note `TIME_NOT_PLACED`, so that whoever words the offer never says that the person gave none. Add a word to one of the lists only where it can make no time longer or shorter than was said, and add a sentence to `tests/test_a_time_beside_a_place.py` with it.
- **A journey is read with its place first as it is with its time first** (contract, section 8.2; mended on 2026-09-27). "Cindermoor Works within 40 minutes" was not plain, so a limit that stood after its place was offered and never applied, and a client that takes what is offered took the guide of it: an area 63 minutes away was ranked under a limit of 40. `_after_its_place` in `grammar.py` reads what is said of a journey after its place, how it is made and the time of it, after a cue, after words that say near or reach a place, and after the name alone. **What makes the time a limit is `FIRM_OF_MINUTES` against its own number, wherever the place stands**: "under" and "in under" make none. The whole name of a place with nothing to lead it in, `_by_a_name`, says a journey only with the time of it: after it in its own part, or as the item straight before it or straight after it, said apart from any place and parted from the name by a mark. `_a_name_alone_is_given_a_time` holds the rest: a name alone is never applied, nor one whose time stands further off, nor one whose time stands before it and after a thing that is some way off, "a park, 10 minutes max, Cindermoor Works", nor one of two such names. A name that is an area's too is a place to reach only where the time stands in its own part, "Foxholt within 30 minutes": apart from it, "Foxholt, 30 minutes max", it may as well say where a person wants to live. Do not let a name alone say a journey without a time: it says nothing of what is wanted of the place. `tests/test_a_time_after_its_place.py` holds it.
- **A house is no flat.** A price is held by the kind of home, and a search to buy holds a flat until a kind is said. So an amount to buy a house of no kind, "a house", "a two bed house", is held against what terraced houses sold for, `DEFAULT_HOUSE` in `spec.py`, and the kind is said to be assumed: a plain prompt is applied with `Assumption.code` `segment`, and in any other the budget is offered for a terraced house first, in an edit that is `inferred`, and for each other kind of house. Where a release holds no price for a terraced house the prompt is not plain and which kind it is is asked, and where it holds a price for no kind of house the budget is said to be missing. `_for_a_house_of_no_kind`, `_for_a_house` and `_Notices._money` in `interpret.py` hold it, and `A_HOUSE` in `grammar.py` the words. It is a default the founder may overturn, and never a flat. Decided on 2026-09-25 (ADR 0012).
- **What a person typed of a home, and Burro cannot hold, is said where the prompt is applied.** A price is held by the kind of home and a rent by the number of bedrooms, so a plain prompt is applied with what the search can hold, and the rest was in no list: the bedrooms of "buying a 3 bed house up to 600k", the kind of house of "renting a terraced house up to £2,000 a month", a studio to buy. Each is said in the words an offer of the home says it in, `BY_KIND`, `BY_BEDROOMS` and `TO_RENT_ALONE`: a suggestion with nothing to choose but to leave it out, which rests on the words that were not held and on no word an edit rests on. `_not_held_of` in `interpret.py` holds it, and what a visit cannot hold with it: an amount, and a wish to move the budget, "somewhere cheaper", which was answered with nothing at all (`NO_STEP_ON_A_VISIT`). The status of a plain prompt is as it was, and is never `suggest`: it would say whether the search is to rent or to buy. **Do not say it as unread.** A model is asked wherever words are left unread, and what it reads is applied by nobody, so a prompt that the rules applied would be applied by nobody. A home is one the grammar took, `_homes_named`, so a wish that is turned round beside it turns nothing of it away: do not ask `_turned_away` of a plain sentence, which looks back past a mark. Two sizes or two kinds of home of which the search holds one, "a 2 bed or a 3 bed", make the prompt not plain: `_of_two_homes`. A house of no kind is a kind to buy that is no flat, so "a flat or a house" names two: it was applied as a flat, and the house was in no list. To rent, a flat or a house beside a room or a studio is another kind: "a room or a flat" was applied as a room. `by_sentence` then holds neither, and what else was said as it was.
- What makes a limit firm is two lists in `vocabulary.py`, one for each kind of limit: `FIRM_OF_MONEY` and `FIRM_OF_MINUTES`. "Max" and "up to" make a budget firm, and "max" and "within" a number of minutes. "Up to" makes no journey firm, and "under", "around" and "maximum" make nothing firm. What caps a number firmly of either kind is `CAPS_FIRMLY`: "at most", "at the very most", "no more than". A budget or a journey that is only noticed is offered as a firm limit where those words stand against its number, and rests on them. Each list is a call made for the founder: change one only with them.
- A number typed with an "m" and no pound sign, "5m", "1.5m", is as likely minutes, metres or miles as millions. A word that caps, "within", "max", is no word of money. It is a budget only where the sentence says what it is for, and is offered or unread anywhere else. `Grammar._an_m_is_money` holds the rule.
- **An amount by the week is never an amount by the month at the same figure.** A rent is held by the month. An amount that is said by the week, "£350 a week", "350pw", "weekly rent of £350", is offered as what it comes to by the month, and its offer says that it was worked out and from what: `by_the_month()` and `worked_out_by_the_month()` in `interpret.py`. The rate is 52 weeks to 12 months, which is what the case `budget-012` says, and is the founder's to change. It is never applied: no word for a week is a word of the grammar, and a plain prompt has nowhere to say that a figure was worked out. An amount by any other period, a year, a fortnight, is not read, since no rule says what it comes to, and its words are unread. An amount by the night is what a stay costs, which Burro holds for no area: it is no budget in any kind of search, `unmet` holds `prices_and_hours` for it, and its words were heard (`_by_the_night`). `paid_by()` says what an amount is paid by, for whoever offers one and for the guard on a model, and `BY_THE_WEEK` and `BY_ANOTHER_PERIOD` in `vocabulary.py` hold the words. **No list of the ways a period is said is ever complete**, so `OF_A_PERIOD` holds every word for a period but the month, and an amount is by no month wherever one stands beside it in words the two lists do not hold: "£350 this week", "£350 per person per week", "£700 every two weeks", "£9,000 for 6 months", "annual rent of £18,000", and the first of "£350 to £400 a week". Each was offered at the figure that was typed. The week and the year are read as they are written with a mark, "p.w.", "/week", "p.a.": `_WRITTEN_AS` in `reading.py`. Add a word for a period there, and never to the grammar. How long a stay is, "for a weekend", "3 nights", is said in words for a period, and none of them is a word of the grammar either: `visit_at` in `grammar.py` reads each as part of the visit it is said of, side by side with it, and anywhere else it is a word the reader does not know.
- **A sentence that says a visit makes the search one, and only where it says nothing else** (contract, section 8.2; decided on 2026-09-26). `VISITS`, `LODGINGS` and `STAYS` in `grammar.py` are the words, closed lists as the words for renting are, and `visit_at` reads them where the grammar places what is said of a home. To stay is as often to remain, so a word for staying says a visit only with how long or with where a visitor stays. In a sentence the grammar does not make, `names_a_visit` in `interpret.py` says whether the words are a visit: not where a word turns them away, where a place is expected, where the visit is somebody else's, or where what stands straight after them says whom or what is visited. "Visiting my mother in Pellam Cross" names a place and no kind of search: it is the gentler reading, and the founder's to overturn. **The name of an area is no rule for the area where the words say that somebody else is there, or that a person wants to be near it, to reach it or to stay in it** (mended on 2026-09-26, and on 2026-09-27 for a cue or a time that stands straight before the name, `_expects_a_place`): `_is_no_rule`. It is a journey to the place where the name is a place's too, and nothing where it is an area's alone, whose words are then unread. A client that takes what is offered looked only there, and left every other area out on a guess. What is typed of a home or of money beside a visit makes no edit and is said with a note, `NO_HOME_ON_A_VISIT` and `NO_BUDGET_ON_A_VISIT`, in a plain sentence and in any other: on a visit an amount alone names no rent and no price by its size (`of_a_visit` in `_Notices`). **A visit weighs nothing of what homes sold for**, `SOLD_FOR` in `catalogue.py`: the reducer turns the edit away as it turns a budget away, a search that becomes a visit drops what it weighed of it, and the reader offers none on a visit or beside the words for one, with the note `NO_PRICE_ON_A_VISIT` where the words are read as nothing else. It is no rejected edit, because route 1 names what the reducer turned away as `not_in_release` among what the data does not hold, and a release may hold rents and prices. Add a word for a visit to the lists, with the count in `tests/test_vocabulary.py`, and a sentence it must not read to `NO_VISIT` in `tests/test_a_visit_in_words.py`. **What is wished of the place may follow a visit with no mark between** (mended on 2026-09-26): "somewhere" opens a wish after the words of a visit, `_after_a_visit` in `grammar.py`, how long the stay is may stand in a part of its own, `stay_alone`, and "away" is part of "a weekend away" and turns nothing away. "Where to stay" is read as a visit and is no phrase of the vocabulary, because "where" must never be a word the reader knows. **A search that is a visit already is never offered a visit again**, whoever chose it: `says_a_visit_again` in `reducer.py`. `tests/test_a_visit_beside_other_words.py` holds the sentences the service was driven with.
- A place that is commuted from, "I commute from", may be where the person lives now. With no time said of it the journey is offered, and never applied.
- **A journey that the words make a walk is a walk.** The words for near that say how a place is reached, "walking distance to", "a short walk to", "can walk to", make the journey to it one on foot: `says_a_walk` in `grammar.py`. It was a journey by public transport, with the way said to be assumed. How long is not said, so the minutes are the usual ones and are said to be assumed: do not make a number of minutes of "walking distance". Two ways said of one journey make the prompt not plain, `_Segment._mode`. In a prompt that is not plain a journey that is noticed holds the way that stands beside its place, in the words the grammar lists and where the grammar would read them: before the name, between the minutes and the name, "a 25 minute walk to", "30 minutes on foot to", and straight after the name. `_way_beside` in `interpret.py` holds it, and `_way_to` what may stand between a time and the place. A way that stands apart from the place is never taken, and nor is either of two. **Nor is a way beside words that may turn it or set another beside it**: "to" before it, but for the speaker's own wish, "unable to walk to"; a word before a way that is named, but for a time, an article and the few words `_BEFORE_A_WAY` lists, "a bus ride to"; more said after the place, of a way after it or before it, "on foot is impossible", "a walk to Pellam Cross is impossible", but that the journey would suit; somebody but the speaker who goes that way, "my ex can walk to"; a word that turns or words of doubt before the way, "not by bike to", "anything but walking distance to"; and a word that joins two ways, "walk or cycle to", "a walk or a short cycle to". `_may_go`, `_may_name_a_way` and `_nothing_more` hold them, and each lets a way stand only after words that are listed: add a word there only where it can say nothing of the way. What says near is passed over whole by `_turned_away`, so that "distance" in "walking distance to" is no wish to stay away.
- **A place to stay away from is never a journey to it.** In a prompt that is not plain a place that is named is offered as a journey, and one press may take one. So a place whose sentence holds a word for far, or a least against a number of minutes, is offered with nothing to choose and the note `NO_STAYING_AWAY`: `STAYS_AWAY`, `AT_LEAST` and `_stays_away`. What says near is passed over, and so is a word for far that a word before it turns round: `NOT_FAR`, `TURNS_ROUND`. A place that may be somebody else's, or was left, is offered with the note `MAY_BE_ANOTHERS`, which keeps every press but its own from it: `SOMEBODY_ELSE`, `OF_THE_HOUSEHOLD`, `LIVES_THERE`, `LEFT_BEHIND` and `_may_be_anothers`. Each list is closed. Add a word to one only where it cannot make a wish to stay away into a journey, and add a sentence to the tests with it. Decided on 2026-09-25 (ADR 0012).
- **A part of a sentence is looked through for two things only where it has 40 words or fewer**: `LONGEST_OF_TWO` in `grammar.py`, which counts a name and a phrase of the lexicon as one word each. A home may stand before a journey or a wish with no word between them, and to find the two is to read the part over again at each word it may be cut at, so a part of three hundred words held the processor for seconds. A longer part is not plain, and what is noticed in it is offered. The longest part that is two things, of every sentence the tests and the evaluation set hold, has 17 words. Do not raise the number without measuring what a sentence of 600 characters then costs, and do not add a reading to `item()` that is tried at every word of a part. `tests/test_a_part_of_many_words.py` holds it.
- **What is worked out of a whole sentence is worked out once.** The times of a sentence are asked for at every name of it, and to work them out is to read the sentence: `_Notices._times_in` in `interpret.py` keeps them while the sentence is noticed. The service is open to anybody, so a sentence that says one name sixty times must cost little more than one that says it twice, which `tests/test_a_name_said_over_and_over.py` holds by counting. Do not call `_times_of` from what is asked of each name.
- Missing data is never filled in. A value that is not known is `None`, and zero never stands for unknown, except as a sentinel inside `Operations`.
- An error message holds paths, rows and codes, and never a value: not a place id, not a name, not what a person typed. `InterpretResult` and `ExplainInput` have no field that can hold user text.
- A rule of `parse_release` is one small function listed in `RULES`. A new rule needs a row in `BROKEN` in `tests/test_release.py` that shows it refusing a release.
- An area says what is known of the name it bears, in `Neighbourhood.named`: the label its publisher gives the area, the sources that write the name, and whether a person has checked it. It is `None` where the area bears no name but its label. Who wrote a name is among the sources of the file of areas, so that the fact of the area cites them, and `references_resolve` refuses a name written by any other. Core chooses no name and holds no rule of naming: the pipeline decides, and core only reads. The fact of an area holds the label, who wrote the name and its state as slots, and its sentence prints none of them.
- `search_areas` finds an area by its name or by another name of it, as `search_places` finds a place. A name that several areas bear is another name of each, so a search finds them all, and `whole_area` takes it for none of them: a word of a sentence that names two areas names neither.
- A release says whether it is a preview, which is a release that is not finished. Only a preview may leave the sources of its journeys or its stations unstated, and only where it holds none. Do not widen that to another file: a figure with no source is no figure. See ADR 0017.
- A release that holds no journey's end, no place to reach, no cost or no station is a preview, and one that says otherwise is refused: `finished_release_is_whole`. A release is not finished on its own word.
- A release that is not made up is served only with what it was built with. `open_served` holds the manifest, the evidence and the lock to the hashes the build wrote. Core reads none of the evidence, and still opens no file: the caller hands it the bytes. Do not let a release that is not made up through `open_served` with nothing beside it. See ADR 0018.
- **A release that was built under another catalogue is read by its own, and is never served.** `open_built` reads it, to be held against another build: it is `open_served` but for the rules that hold a release to the catalogue as core holds it today, which `OF_CORES_CATALOGUE` names. A rule that is added to `RULES` is held by it too, until it is named there in a change a person reads. Call it from nothing that serves a release or checks one, and name no rule there so that a build will be read: a release that does not hold together is refused whatever its catalogue.
- A figure is held to what its unit can be: a percentage from 0 to 100, any other unit not below nought. An area is held to the rest of its own release, and to no place on the map: a test draws its town in open sea under real ids, and a box round London would refuse it.
- Every template must pass the verifier, and every fact kind must have a template that is true of it.
- A comparison in a sentence is literally true of the release: the share of areas strictly beyond, rounded down, with the areas that are level counted and said. Where the share would be nought, as it is of a few areas among more than a hundred, what is said is a count of areas and never nought of anything: how many are level, and where none is, how many are beyond (`few`). Where one area is compared and this one is not it, as an area that is not ranked may be, it is said to be the only other area. Where every area compared is beyond such an area, it is beyond "all 21 areas Burro compared", and never 100% of them (`every`). It is said from the side of its role: a reason from the better side, a trade-off from the worse. The mid-rank percentile is for scoring and is never printed in a sentence.
- The verifier is enough for fixed text and not for a model. `explain()` takes `TemplateExplainer` and refuses any other explainer, in code. Do not loosen that check until the verifier is extended as the contract's section 11 says, and do both in one change. Two tests marked as expected to fail hold the sentences the verifier lets through. A test that plants a sentence does it from a subclass of `TemplateExplainer`.
- The reasons of an explanation are what the person asked for, the three that add most. A usual setting that nobody chose is a reason only where what was asked for gives fewer than three (`_reasons` in `explain.py`, `asked_for()` in `rank.py`).
- A reason never states a shortfall: a home over budget and a journey over its cap are never given as a reason, whatever they add to the score. And a trade-off is never something the area does well: it is a component worth less than `TRADE_OFF_MAX_UTILITY`, or a shortfall. It is worse than most and bad in itself: a walk, a time or a distance at or under its figure in `NEVER_A_TRADE_OFF` is never one, however many areas are closer. That table is in the catalogue, and a test holds it to every feature in minutes or metres. What lies between the two thresholds is in no sentence. An area that does nothing badly has no trade-off.

- An area with no figure for a thing that was asked for stands below every area that has one. It is ranked, and its fit is worked out on the rest as before. `asked_for()` in `rank.py` says what was asked: everything but a usual setting, which is a feature the tenure weighs by default that stands at the weight nobody chose, whole or given way. It is read from the weight and never from a provenance. A journey, a budget and a vibe are always asked for.
- The character that counts is covered on its own. An area with a figure for under half of the features and vibes of a search, by weight, is not ranked, whatever is known of its journeys and its cost: it is `unranked` with `character_unknown`, and says what it lacks. What counts as character never depends on a provenance, because two specs with one canonical form rank the same.
- **Household income is apart, as the census is.** `income.py` holds what an area's page shows of what its households are estimated to have. No route ranks on it, compares on it or explains by it: it is no feature, vibe, cost or fact, `Release` returns none, and no other module of core imports it. `tests/test_income.py` holds each. - **The census is apart.** `census.py` holds the figures an area's page shows of who lived there, and nothing that ranks, explains, compares or reads a sentence can be handed one: `Release` returns none, no other module imports it, and it is told of a release only its id, whether it is made up, and its areas. Do not import it here, do not export it from `burro_core`, and give no `FeatureId`, `TagId` or `FactKind` the name of what is shown and never ranked on: ethnic group, religion, country of birth. A measure of age or of households that feeds a vibe is a feature of the catalogue, read from the release, and never from a census. `tests/test_census.py` holds each of these.
- What is served of a census figure is worked out in `panel()` and nowhere else: a share in whole per cents, its count, and the whole city's share beside it. A share under 1 in 100 is said in words, with no count. The rows keep the order of the file. No word of `CENSUS_2021` or `MADE_UP` says what a figure means: `NEVER_SAID` is the list, and a test reads every word against it.
- Tests run offline and share their releases through `tests/support.py`, imported as `from .support import ...`. Every name in it is made up, and was looked up in an encyclopaedia on 2026-09-23. A name that was found to be real is held in `packages/pipeline/tests/test_synthetic_release.py`, where a test looks for it in every file, the name of a constant included. `tests/test_fixture.py` reads the committed synthetic release, for what must hold of the release that is served. The test reads the bytes; core still opens no file.
