## The words of the reply

The words of the reply are not printed here. They stay with the reply, which the founder holds ([ADR 0007](../../docs/adr/0007-no-solicitor.md)).

Three owners answered that day, as the founder reports: parkrun, the GIS team of the Greater London Authority, and Arts Council England.

## What the permission covers

- It covers what was asked, and no more.
- A change of use means asking the Greater London Authority again, before the change is made.
- The Greater London Authority is due a credit wherever a figure from the layer is shown. It is given in Burro's words until the owner publishes its own.

## What this note cannot say

- What the Greater London Authority wrote.
- Whether the reply sets a limit, an end date or a form of credit.
- Whether the reply speaks of the imagery, the height data and the Ordnance Survey data the layer was made from. An owner can give only the rights it holds.

## What would settle it

The founder files a dated note of the reply in this folder, written from the reply itself. It gives the day Burro asked, the route, what the owner allowed, and any limit it sets. It quotes nothing of the reply, and holds no person's name and no address. The credit is given in Burro's words until the owner publishes its own.

Until then the entry is `gated`, and the gate refuses it for every use.
