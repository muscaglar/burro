## What it states, and what it does not

- It states who made the figures, that Crown copyright and database rights are held in them, the year 2020, and a licence number of Ordnance Survey.
- It states no licence of the table, and no term of use: nothing that says what a reader may or may not do with it.
- It does not name the Open Government Licence. The page it is published on does.
- It states no month and no day, and no census.

## What this note cannot say
